Frequently Asked Questions

About Virtuse
What is Virtuse?
Virtuse is an independent comparison hub for Bitcoin-only services. Founded in 2018 and Bitcoin-only since 2022, it links you to regulated partners for buying, mining, borrowing, custody, treasury, tax and automation. You buy, store and borrow on the partner's own platform, under its licence. Virtuse is operated by Virtuse Wealth Management, a.s. (see About).
Is Virtuse an exchange?
No. Virtuse doesn't operate an exchange, hold your funds or take custody of your Bitcoin. We are a comparison and access layer: we list licensed providers in each category and link you directly to them.
Why Bitcoin only?
Virtuse covers Bitcoin and nothing else. One asset keeps the comparisons simple and lets us go deep on what matters for Bitcoin: custody, fees, tax and inheritance. No altcoins, no tokens.
Our Hub Services
What services can I access through Virtuse?
Seven service categories, each with hand-picked partners: Buy Bitcoin (MiCA-licensed CASPs like 21bitcoin, Kraken, and ByBit EU), Mining (hosted mining with OneMiners, Abundant Mines, Power Mining), Loans (Bitcoin-backed borrowing via Firefish: keep your BTC, get cash), Custody (Trezor, Ledger, and Blockstream Jade hardware wallets), Treasury (institutional custody with Coinbase Institutional, Sygnum, BitGo), Tax (Divly, Blockpit, CoinTracking, Koinly), Trading Bots (Coinrule, Cryptohopper, RevenueBot), plus free Bitcoin Data tools: live dashboard, retirement calculator, rainbow chart, and more.
How does the hub work?
Three steps: pick a service, compare our vetted partners side by side, and click through to the partner's regulated platform. Everything after that (account, verification, transactions) happens directly with the partner. Your Bitcoin stays under your control.
How do you choose partners?
We prioritize EU licensing (MiCA/MiCAR where applicable), security track record, Bitcoin-friendliness, and fair fees. Partners that don't meet the bar don't get listed.
Security
Does Virtuse ever hold my Bitcoin or my keys?
No. Virtuse never holds your funds or your keys. All transactions run on regulated partner platforms. We only store what you give us yourself, for example your email address when you subscribe to the Brief or join the CFO waitlist. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
How should I store my Bitcoin?
Many long-term holders use a hardware wallet: the keys are generated offline and never leave the device. Our Custody page compares the hardware wallets we list (Trezor, Ledger, Blockstream Jade). For larger holdings and companies, our Treasury partners offer institutional custody. The choice is yours; this is not investment advice.
Are the partners regulated?
Buying partners are MiCA/MiCAR-licensed EU crypto-asset service providers, for example Kraken via the Central Bank of Ireland, and ByBit EU and 21bitcoin via the Austrian FMA. Treasury partners include regulated institutions like Sygnum (Swiss banking licence) and BitGo.
Fees
What does Virtuse charge?
Nothing. Browsing, comparing and clicking through to partners is free. Some partners pay a referral fee when you sign up through our link. You pay the same price either way.
What fees will I pay at partners?
Each partner sets its own pricing, and prices change. The Bitcoin Fee Index compares buying costs with a source and a check date for every fee. Always confirm current pricing on the partner's site before you sign up.
Trading
Can I trade on Virtuse?
Trading happens on our partners' platforms, not on Virtuse. For spot buying and selling, use a Buy Bitcoin partner. For automated strategies (DCA, grid, rebalancing), our Bots partners connect to your exchange account via API keys. It is non-custodial, so the bot never holds your coins.
Do you support altcoin trading?
No. Virtuse covers Bitcoin services only. Some partners offer other assets on their own platforms, but everything we compare is Bitcoin.
Deposits
How do I deposit money to buy Bitcoin?
Directly on the partner platform you choose, typically via SEPA bank transfer or card with EU partners like 21bitcoin, Kraken, or ByBit EU. Virtuse never receives or processes your money.
Can I set up recurring purchases?
Yes. Several partners, for example 21bitcoin and Kraken, support recurring purchases (DCA). Fees for recurring buys differ by partner: see the Bitcoin Fee Index and the partner's own site.
Withdrawals
How do I withdraw my Bitcoin?
From the partner platform to any wallet address you control, for example your own hardware wallet. Miners receive rewards paid out daily, directly to a wallet address they control at all times.
Are there withdrawal limits or fees?
These are set by each partner and vary by platform, verification level, and network conditions. Check the partner's current terms; Virtuse adds nothing on top.
KYC & Verification
Does Virtuse require KYC?
No. Because Virtuse never touches your funds, there's nothing to verify with us. Identity verification happens once, on the regulated partner platform where you transact.
What will partners ask for?
Licensed EU CASPs follow MiCA and AML rules: typically a government ID and proof of address, occasionally source-of-funds for larger amounts. Requirements vary by partner and country.
Are there services without KYC?
Yes. Tools that never touch your money don't need it: hardware wallets (Trezor, Ledger), tax software (Divly is explicitly privacy-first with no KYC), and all our free Bitcoin Data tools work without any account at all.
