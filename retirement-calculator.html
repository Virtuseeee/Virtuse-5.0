How it works

Step 01

Enter your plan

Your age, when you want to retire, your Bitcoin stack, your monthly savings and your yearly spending in today's euros.

Step 02

Pick a growth scenario

Conservative, Base or Optimistic, or your own rate. These are hypothetical scenarios, not forecasts.

Step 03

See if it lasts

Your savings buy Bitcoin until you retire. From then on, your spending is withdrawn every year until your life expectancy.

Questions and answers

Is this a forecast?

No. Bitcoin's price is volatile and the growth rates are hypothetical scenarios. Past performance doesn't guarantee future results, and Bitcoin can fall in value.

What does "BTC needed today" mean?

The smallest stack you would need today to retire at your chosen age without saving anything more, under the same growth and inflation assumptions.

Are taxes included?

No. Selling Bitcoin can be taxable in your country. The Bitcoin tax guides explain the rules for 13 EU countries.

Does Virtuse hold my Bitcoin?

No. The calculator runs in your browser and stores nothing. Virtuse never holds your keys or funds.
