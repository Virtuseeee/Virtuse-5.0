Questions. Read these before you buy hardware.

Is mining still profitable?

It depends on Bitcoin's price, network difficulty and your electricity cost, and it can be negative. Run your own numbers with a mining calculator before you buy hardware. We make no profitability promises.

Who holds my mining rewards?

Payouts go to a Bitcoin address you control. Virtuse never holds coins, keys or money.
