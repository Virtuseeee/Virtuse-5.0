**Pricing rationale (internal, not page copy):**
- Free is the funnel: Brief + dashboards + guides + Partner Finder build the list; affiliate revenue
  funds it.
- Pro at coffee price; pays for itself via member discounts and avoided mistakes (fee tier, missed
  tax exemption, a liquidation seen coming).
- Pro Max priced against the cost of getting custody/inheritance wrong (100 % of the stack);
  €1,490 ≈ 1.5 % of a €100k holding, once a year, whole family.
- Anchor: show Pro Max first on desktop; expect most buyers on Pro.
- Launch offer: first 500 Pro members lock €59/yr for life.

## Small print (under the table)

> Virtuse provides information, comparison tools and education. We do not provide advice on
> crypto-assets, investment, tax or legal advice, and we do not recommend any specific transaction
> or provider. Partner rankings are sorted by objective criteria (fees, country availability,
> custody type). Some links are affiliate links; this never changes the order.

**Reviewer note — check this against the live tool:** the live Partner Finder orders results by
fixed rules based on the visitor's answers (`recommend()` push order), **not** by a fee sort. Its
own disclaimer says "Results depend only on your answers and never on commission." The small print's
"sorted by objective criteria (fees, country availability, custody type)" may overstate this.
Suggested alternative: *"Results are filtered by your answers using fixed rules; commission never
changes them."*

## Open issues already known

1. **Pulse claim** "smarter than 99% of holders" is unprovable (UCPD; MiCA marketing standard).
   The user chose to keep it; offered alternatives: "the Bitcoin newsletter for holders who want to
   understand, not just own" / "know more than most holders ever will".
2. **"Partners regulated in the EU"** (hero trust line elsewhere on the offer page) is not true for
   every partner: mining hosting is unregulated, Firefish lending is not a MiCA service, bot platforms
   need checking. Check all 22 against ESMA's CASP register (transition ended 1 July 2026).
3. **Q&A and priority email** need written answering rules (general education only, no "you should",
   answer with the comparison table).
4. **Affiliate disclosure** must be visible on category pages before any "ranking guarantee" goes live.
5. A lawyer (ideally Slovak) should confirm that Partner Finder counts as comparison rather than advice,
   and confirm the Pro Max scope.
