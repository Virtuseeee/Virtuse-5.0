# Partner discount outreach — templates

Reusable templates for asking Virtuse's referral partners to add a
promotional discount for Virtuse-referred clients, to support sales.
Drafted 2026-09-02.

## Why this framing works

1. **Lead with what you're giving, not what you're asking.** Frame it as
   "you get access to warm, pre-qualified demand; in exchange, sweeten
   the offer for that specific channel" — not a cold "please give us a
   discount." The discount funds itself if it converts.
2. **Quantify reach.** Use real numbers: 18,000+ Virtuse Report
   subscribers, plus the specific page's traffic if you have it. A
   partner's sales team can size a deal against a number, not a vague
   "our audience."
3. **Make the ask concrete.** Propose a specific structure (e.g. "X% off
   for the first 3 months for anyone who signs up via a Virtuse
   link/code") so they can say yes to a fully-formed idea instead of
   designing one themselves.
4. **Offer a clear value exchange**, not just a request: featured
   placement on the relevant landing page, a mention in the weekly
   Virtuse Report, a unique tracked link/code so they can see the ROI
   themselves, first right of refusal on future co-marketing.
5. **Make tracking effortless for them** — a unique promo code or
   tracked link per partner from day one proves the deal's ROI, which is
   what gets it renewed or expanded later.
6. **Time-box it.** "For our next 90 days of push" is lower-risk to say
   yes to than an open-ended ask, and creates urgency.

**Grounding note**: this isn't cold outreach for most partners — Kraken,
Bybit EU, Invity, and 21bitcoin (on
[`buy-bitcoin.html`](../Kimi_Agent_Virtuse%20MiCA%20Partners/buy-bitcoin.html))
and OneMiners, Abundant Mines, and Power Mining (on
[`mining.html`](../Kimi_Agent_Virtuse%20MiCA%20Partners/mining.html))
already have live referral links with Virtuse. The ask is "let's add a
discount to convert more of the traffic we're already sending you," not
"become a partner." **Internal note, don't put this in the email
itself**: OneMiners' live link already carries a Virtuse-specific
discount code (`?discount=virtuse_buy`) — no other current partner does
— useful precedent to know when prioritizing who to push hardest, but
don't name-drop one partner's deal to another.

## Buy Bitcoin variant

For: Kraken, Bybit EU, Invity, 21bitcoin

---

**Subject: Adding a Virtuse-exclusive offer to our Buy Bitcoin partnership**

Hi [Name],

[Partner Name] is already live on our [Buy Bitcoin page](https://virtuse.com/buy-bitcoin.html) via our referral link, and we'd like to make that traffic convert better for both of us.

Right now visitors see [Partner Name] listed alongside a few other licensed CASPs, with no extra incentive to choose you over them. I'd like to change that: a **limited-time discount exclusively for Virtuse-referred signups** — something like [a fee discount on their first trade / a reduced spread for the first 30 days / a small welcome bonus] — visible right on the partner card.

In exchange, we'll:
- Give [Partner Name] top placement on the Buy Bitcoin page for the duration of the offer
- Promote it directly to our 18,000+ Virtuse Report subscribers
- Track it through our existing referral link, so you see exactly what it drives beyond what you're already getting

Compliance-conscious investors are exactly who reads Virtuse, and a visible, time-boxed offer is what turns "another licensed exchange" into "the one I click." Happy to keep it simple — a discount code or a dedicated landing page, whatever's least friction on your end.

Would you be open to a short call this week to work out the details?

Best,
[Your name]
Virtuse

## Before sending, tailor per partner

- **The offer structure** — percentage off, waived fee, or extended
  trial lands differently depending on the partner's business.
- **The specific page/placement** you're offering — reference the actual
  page so it reads concrete, not generic.
- **Personalize the opener** if there's any existing contact history —
  a templated-feeling first line is the fastest way to get ignored.
