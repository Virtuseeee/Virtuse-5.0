How Bitcoin did in July in every year since 2011: the return from the last close of June to the last close of July, the average and the median, how many years closed higher, and where July ranks among the 12 months.

July closed higher in 11 of 16 years (2011–2026), more often than the average month (57% of all months since August 2010). The average (+8.3%) and the median (+8.0%) are close, so no single year dominates. By median it ranks 5th of the 12 months, by average 9th. It has been positive in each of the last 3 years (2024–2026).
