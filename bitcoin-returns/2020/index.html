Bitcoin Returns 2020 month by month

Bitcoin's return in each month of 2020, the price at the start and end of the year, its high, low and deepest fall from a high, and how 2020 compares with 2019.

Data as of 20 Oct 2026 · US dollars · CoinMetrics daily reference rate

Return 2020+305%
End of 2019$7,167
End of 2020$29,023
Max drawdown−52.1%to 12 Mar 2020
Months higher8/12

Bitcoin rose 305% in 2020, from $7,167 at the end of 2019 to $29,023 at the end of 2020. 8 of 12 months closed higher; the best was December (+47.6%), the worst March (−25.0%). The deepest fall during the year was −52.1%, from the high of 14 Feb 2020 to 12 Mar 2020; the highest close was $29,023 on 31 Dec 2020. That ranks 4th of 15 full years since 2011, after +94.4% in 2019.
