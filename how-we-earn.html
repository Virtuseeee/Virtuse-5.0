How Virtuse earns money.

Where our income comes from, which links pay us a commission and how partners are ordered.

Our income

Virtuse earns from the Virtuse CFO subscription, from sales of full reports and from commissions from some of the providers we link to.

These are currently the following providers:

  • 21bitcoin
  • ByBit EU
  • Kraken
  • Crypto.com
  • Invity
  • Blockstream
  • Ledger
  • Trezor
  • Firefish
  • Koinly
  • Blockpit
  • CoinTracking
  • Divly
  • Coinrule
  • Cryptohopper
  • RevenueBot
  • Abundant Mines
  • OneMiners
  • PowerMining

We receive a commission if you sign up or use a partner's service through our link; your price does not change. Partner links are always labelled. Virtuse Verdict and the fee audit contain no partner links, and commissions have no influence on their results. Nothing on this website is a recommendation to use a particular service. Before deciding, compare the terms of several providers.

We also list BitGo, Coinbase (Institutional) and Sygnum, without a commission link.

How we label partner links

Next to a partner link we show this text:

“Partner link – if you sign up, Virtuse may receive a commission. Your price does not change.”

Order in Partner Finder

Partner Finder shows the partners that match your answers. Their order comes from fixed rules based on the answers you give (goal, country, experience, custody, amount), applied in a fixed order.

Commissions have no influence on whether a partner is shown or on its position. There is no paid placement.
