Privacy Policy

How Virtuse handles your data, in plain language. Short version: we run a non-custodial hub, we collect almost nothing, and what little we do collect is explained below. Effective 1 November 2026.

Who We Are · What We Collect · How We Use It · Cookies · Third Parties · Transfers · Your Rights · Contact · Verdict & CFO · Ecosystem
1. Who We Are
Data controller
The controller of personal data collected through this website, for the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), is Virtuse Wealth Management, a.s., registered office Borinka 65, 900 32 Borinka, Slovakia, IČO (company ID): 36 709 042, DIČ (tax ID): 2022293042, IČ DPH (VAT ID): SK2022293042, registered in the Commercial Register of the Municipal Court Bratislava III, Section: Sa, Insert No. 4452/B ("Virtuse", "we", "us"). Data protection contact: info@virtuse.com (please put "Data protection" in the subject line).
What this website is — and what it is not
Virtuse is a non-custodial hub for Bitcoin-only services. We do not operate an exchange, hold client funds or keys, open accounts, or perform identity verification (KYC) on this website. When you use a partner service (buying, mining, lending, custody, tax, or trading bots), you contract directly with that partner on their own platform, and that partner is an independent data controller for anything you share with them. This dramatically limits the personal data we ourselves process.
2. What We Collect
Data you give us
Newsletter email address — if you subscribe to the Virtuse Brief or join the waitlist for Virtuse CFO or the Verdict full report, we process the email address you provide, based on your consent. Apart from Virtuse Verdict and Virtuse CFO (section 9) and the Ecosystem application form (section 10), that is the only personal data this website asks you for. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in every email, and we will delete your address from the mailing list.
Data collected automatically
Usage data — like virtually every website, our hosting infrastructure records standard server logs: IP address, browser type and version, pages visited, timestamps, and referring pages. We use these solely for security, abuse prevention, and keeping the site running. We do not build visitor profiles and we do not use this data for advertising.
Data we do not collect
Apart from Virtuse Verdict and Virtuse CFO (section 9): no account data, no identity documents, no financial or payment information, no wallet addresses, no transaction history. Our free tools (the Bitcoin Data dashboard, Retirement Calculator, Rainbow Chart, Root-Scale Cycles, BTC Dominance, 200 WMA, Fear & Greed, Trading Volume) run entirely in your browser — the figures you enter into the Retirement Calculator never leave your device.
3. How & Why We Use Data
Purposes and legal bases (Art. 6 GDPR)
We process personal data only for: (a) delivering the newsletter you asked for — legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), withdrawable at any time; (b) operating, securing and debugging the website — legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) in keeping our service available and safe; and (c) complying with legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)) where EU or Slovak law requires it. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.
Referral relationships
Virtuse earns referral fees when you sign up with a partner via our links. This works through standard referral URLs — we do not pass your personal data to partners, and partners do not send us your personal data back. At most, partners report aggregate, non-identifying statistics (e.g., number of sign-ups) for commission settlement.
4. Cookies & Local Storage
What we set
Necessary storage (always on): your language, theme and cookie choice, kept in your browser so the site works. It needs no consent. Visit statistics without cookies (always on): Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views without cookies and without any identifier, and stores nothing in your browser, so it needs no consent. Analytics (only with your consent): Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, sets cookies (such as _ga, kept for up to 2 years) to count visits and show us which pages are useful. Marketing (only with your consent): Google Ads cookies to measure campaigns and show our ads to people who visited the site. Until you choose, both stay off. We use Google Consent Mode, so before consent Google receives only cookieless signals, without advertising identifiers. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive). Google may process this data outside the EEA under the safeguards described in section 6.
Managing cookies
You can accept, reject or change your choice at any time. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it and does not affect processing that happened before. We ask again after 12 months. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site stays fully usable without them.
5. Third-Party Services
Services your browser talks to on our pages
Some features load content or data directly from third parties. When they do, those providers technically receive your IP address as part of the request, under their own privacy policies: Google Fonts (typefaces), Cloudflare Web Analytics (visit statistics without cookies, see section 4), jsDelivr (animation library), WordPress/virtuse.com (blog articles and images — our own service), mempool.space, CoinGecko, Binance, and Alternative.me (live, read-only market and network data for our Bitcoin Data tools), and bitcoinwave.net (the embedded Root-Scale Cycles chart). These requests carry no identifiers from us — no login, no cookie of ours, nothing beyond what any webpage request contains.
Links to partners and other sites
Our pages link to partner platforms (e.g., Kraken, 21bitcoin, Bybit EU, Firefish, Trezor, Ledger, and others) and to press coverage. Once you leave our site, the destination's privacy policy governs. Partners performing regulated services will conduct their own KYC under MiCA and EU anti-money-laundering rules — that data goes to them, never through us. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of every service you sign up for.
6. Retention & International Transfers
How long we keep data
Newsletter and waitlist addresses: until you withdraw your consent; you can unsubscribe in every email. Operational and server logs (Cloudflare Workers Logs): deleted automatically after 7 days. Google Analytics 4 (only with your consent): events 2 months, the _ga cookie up to 2 years. Support and complaint emails: 2 years after the matter is handled. We keep nothing "just in case".
Transfers outside the EEA
We are an EU-based operator (in Slovakia). Some service providers may, however, process data outside the European Economic Area. For our hosting provider Cloudflare (USA and global network), the transfer relies on Cloudflare, Inc.'s certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795) and, in support, on standard contractual clauses. In other cases we rely on the safeguards of Chapter V GDPR: primarily the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, or an adequacy decision where one exists (for example, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for certified US providers). You may request a copy of the relevant safeguards via the contact below.
7. Your Rights
Your GDPR rights
If you are in the EEA you have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), objection to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21), and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior processing (Art. 7(3)). Given how little we collect, most requests concern the newsletter — and the unsubscribe link resolves those instantly.
Exercising your rights and complaints
Email info@virtuse.com (subject "Data protection"). We respond within one month, as GDPR requires, and may ask you to verify your identity first. If you believe we have mishandled your data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov Slovenskej republiky (Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic), Hraničná 12, 820 07 Bratislava 27, www.dataprotection.gov.sk, or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence.
Children
This website is not directed at anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.
8. Security, Changes & Contact
Security of data
We apply proportionate technical and organisational measures (TLS everywhere, minimal data collection by design, access controls). No internet transmission is 100% secure — which is one more reason our architecture avoids collecting sensitive data in the first place.
Changes to this policy
We may update this Privacy Policy as the website or the law evolves. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date; material changes will be flagged prominently on the site or via the newsletter before they take effect.
Contact us
Questions about this policy or your data: info@virtuse.com (subject "Data protection"), or by post to Virtuse Wealth Management, a.s., Borinka 65, 900 32 Borinka, Slovakia.
9. Virtuse Verdict and Virtuse CFO
Briefly and truthfully
  • We do not store the text of your decision in Verdict. It is processed only in working memory during the evaluation and is not written to a database or to Virtuse's logs. We store it only if you buy the full report or save the evaluation in your Virtuse CFO account (see the table). For the evaluation, we send the text to the AI provider Anthropic (USA), which automatically deletes inputs and outputs within 30 days; if its automated systems flag them as a violation of its usage policies, it may keep them for up to 2 years. Anthropic does not use API data to train models.
  • To protect against abuse, we count requests from your IP address (limit of 10 evaluations per day). We do not store the IP address in readable form, only as a one-way fingerprint (a hash with a salt that changes every 24 hours). The counter is deleted automatically after 24 hours.
  • Our hosting provider Cloudflare processes technical data (in particular the IP address) in operational logs, which are deleted automatically after 7 days. We do not write the text of your reasoning to the logs.
  • Only the band and the weakest area go into analytics, never the text of your reasoning.
  • When you make a purchase, we keep payment data and tax documents for 10 years, as the Accounting Act requires; we delete the content of a full report bought without an account 30 days after purchase.
  • The exchange export in the fee audit is processed only in your browser and is not sent to us.
  • Please do not write names, contact details, account numbers, wallet addresses, health data or other sensitive data in the text.

Special categories of data
We do not need or ask for data on health, religion, ethnic origin or other special categories (Art. 9 GDPR). Before the text is sent for evaluation, an automatic filter is applied that replaces recognised terms with "[removed]". The AI is instructed not to mention or evaluate such data. If you include it despite the warning, it is processed only temporarily, to the extent necessary for the evaluation, and is not stored at Virtuse.
Transfers to third countries
  1. Anthropic (USA): the transfer takes place on the basis of the EU standard contractual clauses (Commission Decision (EU) 2021/914), which form part of Anthropic's data processing agreement, and supplementary measures.
  2. Cloudflare (USA and global network): the transfer relies on Cloudflare, Inc.'s certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795) and, in support, on standard contractual clauses.
Automated assessment
The score in Verdict is an automated assessment of the completeness of the text you enter. It is not a decision with legal effects or a similarly significant effect on you within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR, it is not used to assess creditworthiness, and we do not provide it to third parties.
Your rights
You have the right of access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, the right to object to processing based on legitimate interest, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. Send requests to info@virtuse.com; we will handle them within one month. Please note that we do not store the text of your reasoning in Verdict, so we cannot retrieve or provide it afterwards. You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov SR (Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic), Hraničná 12, 820 07 Bratislava 27, www.dataprotection.gov.sk, or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence.
Changes
We will inform you about material changes on the website, and CFO users also by email.
10. Bitcoin Ecosystem applications
What we process and why
If you apply to have a project listed in the Virtuse Bitcoin Ecosystem (virtuse.com/ecosystem/promote/), we process the details you enter: your name and email address, the project's name, website, description, category, countries served, licence details and repository link if you give them, whether you are interested in a sponsored placement, your message and the form language. We use them only to review the application, contact you about it and, if the project is accepted, prepare and maintain the listing. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a possible agreement (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in running a curated directory (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
How your application is handled
The application is sent by email to our team through Resend (email service, processor) and is not stored in a database. To protect the form against spam we use Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks technical data from your browser and your IP address (Cloudflare, processor; transfers as described in section 6). To limit abuse we count submissions per IP address for one hour and remember the email address for 24 hours, so that only one confirmation is sent.
What we publish
We publish only information about the project (name, website, description and our own short verdict), never your name or email address. Paid or featured placements are always labelled.
How long we keep it
Retention: applications we decline are deleted 12 months after our decision. For accepted projects we keep the application and correspondence for as long as the listing is live and for 2 years after it is removed. You can withdraw an application or ask us to delete your details at any time at info@virtuse.com (subject "Data protection"); your rights are described in section 7.
