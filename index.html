Which exchange won't rug me?

Mt. Gox, FTX, Celsius — billions in customer funds have vanished in exchange collapses since 2014. We only list MiCA-licensed CASPs, vetted for custody, insurance, and jurisdiction before they ever reach this page.

What happens to my BTC when I die?

An estimated one-fifth of all Bitcoin ever mined is already lost forever, much of it locked behind a key only one person ever held. Self-custody without a succession plan is a ticking clock, not a safeguard.

Plan your Bitcoin inheritance

What do I owe the taxman?

Every sale, swap, and mining reward is a taxable event in most EU jurisdictions — and the rules differ by country. Get it wrong and the bill, plus penalties, lands years later, not now.
