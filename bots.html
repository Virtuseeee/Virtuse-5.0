Questions. Read these before you connect a bot.

Can the bot withdraw my Bitcoin?

Not with a trade-only API key. Never enable withdrawal permissions for a bot. Your coins stay in your exchange account, and the bot never holds your funds.

Can I lose money with a bot?

Yes. Automation doesn't remove market risk, and backtests don't guarantee future results. Start small and paper-trade first.

Is DCA with a bot taxable?

Buying is usually not a taxable event; selling or swapping may be. Our country tax guides summarise the rules in 13 EU countries. This is general information, not tax advice. Bitcoin tax guides →

Is my Bitcoin safe on the exchange?

The bot never holds your funds, but the exchange does: it holds your coins and keys. Keep only what you trade on the exchange and move the rest to self-custody. Self-custody options →
