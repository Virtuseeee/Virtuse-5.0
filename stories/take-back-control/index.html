Take Back Control

By Ras Vasilisin Policy desk 28 May 2020 · 11:07 UTC Block — 2 min
Share

The coronavirus has ushered in a new world that looks weirder and weirder every day. 

Most of the world is still sitting at home watching the economy collapse. 

At the same time, governments together with big tech are doubling down on Orwellian-style surveillance. 

The US Senate almost passed a spying bill for your browsing history. Read here.  

But a few people know that the FBI can open your browser without any warrant, since 2001.

This includes anyone’s browsing history, even the 96% of us living outside of the US. 

The Chinese-style control became the new normal, globally. 

And people are begging for it. 

The problem being is, as Orwell explained: 

“The choice for mankind lies between freedom and happiness and for the great bulk of mankind, happiness is better.”

But for any free-minded person, privacy is incredibly important. 

I spent years doing business in China.

And I experienced their elaborate surveillance network, with all the cameras, the great firewall, and digital technologies. 

And personally, I don’t want to live in a world like that. 

There is no more important time than now to start paying attention to what can be tracked of your stuff.

Fortunately, there’s a simple SOLUTION (at least a partial one)…

Stop using any of the major browsers (Chrome, Opera or Safari)! 

The best one to use is the Brave browser. 

It does not track ANYTHING you do. 

When you browse the internet, your browser measures and tracks your web activity. 

Brave’s browser blocks tracking and instead allows the user to choose to participate in a “clean” model which is private by default. 

It basically is a Google Chrome but without the tracking. 

But wait there’s more…

This privacy-focused browser can also make you money.

They have their own coin, BAT (Basic Attention Token). 

And, if an advertiser pays Brave a dollar, you get 70 cents of it, in BAT equivalent. 

How can you switch?

I was surprised by how easy it is. 

It took me a minute to switch from Chrome.

And my digital data cannot be tracked anymore.

FINAL POINT

So unless you have a desire for constant tracking you have to get Brave.
