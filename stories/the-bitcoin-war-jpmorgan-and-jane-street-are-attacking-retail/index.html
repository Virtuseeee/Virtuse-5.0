THE BITCOIN WAR: JPMorgan and Jane Street Are Attacking Retail

By Ras Vasilisin Markets desk 18 Dec 2025 · 13:39 UTC Block — 2 min
Share

This is the time when the true legends are made

Do you know the difference between a conspiracy theory and the truth? 

About six months.

Lately, the evidence has been piling up: what we’re seeing in the markets isn’t just organic movement—it’s targeted manipulation.

Here are the smoking-gun examples that confirm the cards are being stacked against the small-time investor:

The Surgical Strike on MicroStrategy

Just before MicroStrategy announced a massive $835 million BTC purchase, the prime brokerage division of JPMorgan drastically hiked the margin requirements for MSTR stock. They moved it from 50% all the way up to 95%. This surgically precise move immediately choked the leverage of hedge funds, forcing them to sell MSTR and causing the stock price to instantly collapse. The attack was hyper-focused; similar measures were not applied to other volatile stocks, like Tesla.

Jane Street’s Wall Street Shenanigans

Jane Street (one of the world’s largest market makers) recently timed massive sell-offs worth $2.5 billion in BlackRock’s IBIT ETF. Right at the opening bell of the US markets. This doesn’t sound like a free market; it sounds like classic Wall Street machination.

The Paradox: When MicroStrategy announced a huge Bitcoin purchase recently, the price of BTC didn’t soar—it dropped. That’s simply not how supply and demand is supposed to work.

The Classic Pump and Dump Playbook

Ever experience a lightning-fast surge in price followed by a brutal, sudden crash?

 Analysts call it a “liquidity grab” or the infamous “pump and dump.”

It’s the oldest trick in the book. An institution pushes the price up, provoking over-leveraged retail investors to buy in. They then harvest the liquidity at the peak and trigger the crash, liquidating all those new long positions in the process.

The Bottom Line: Why is This Happening?

One of Mahatma Gandhi’s most famous quotes rings true here: 

“First they ignore you, then they laugh at you, then they attack you, and then you win.”

Wall Street has moved past the mocking and ignoring phase; they are now in the full attack phase.

They have no other choice. They know they can’t destroy Bitcoin, so they must dominate it and control its financial infrastructure. And to do that, they need your Bitcoin.

These machinations are designed to shake out the “weak hands.”

This manipulation isn’t a verdict against Bitcoin; it’s proof of just how precious and dangerous it is to them. Financial parasites only attack Bitcoin because they fear it. They know Bitcoin has the power to destroy and replace them.

But let’s be clear: Bitcoin has already survived much worse attacks—without ever changing its protocol.

A Final Message

You have two choices: You must either become a true Bitcoiner with diamond hands on your position, or you should sell your Bitcoin and get out of the game forever.

This is the time when the true legends are made.
