The Virtuse Bitcoin Ecosystem directory is curated by hand. We do not accept automatic submissions. If you believe your project belongs here, please email us or send the application form below. We review every application personally and may decline any application without giving a reason.

Application

Criteria. What every listing must meet.

  1. Bitcoin only. You support bitcoin (on-chain and/or Lightning) and do not list, sell or promote other cryptocurrencies or tokens.
  2. Relevant to Europe. You serve users in the EU/EEA or Switzerland.
  3. Regulated where required. If you provide crypto-asset services in the EU, you hold a MiCA (CASP) authorisation or an applicable transitional licence.
  4. Live and maintained, with a working public website.
  5. Honest: no yield promises and no undisclosed custody.
