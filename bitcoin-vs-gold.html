Questions and answers

Can I buy gold on Virtuse?

No. Virtuse is a Bitcoin-only hub, not an exchange, and it does not sell gold, silver, commodities or any other asset. This page only compares returns. If you want to buy Bitcoin, the Buy Bitcoin page lists vetted partners.

Where does the data come from?

Prices come from Yahoo Finance: the ETFs GLD, SLV, USO, CPER, PPLT and DBC for returns, the front-month futures GC=F, SI=F, CL=F, HG=F and PL=F for prices and ratios, and BTC-USD for Bitcoin. A script rebuilds the dataset every day after the US close; if a source fails, the page keeps the last good data and shows its date.

Why ETFs for returns and futures for the ratio?

An ETF's adjusted price is a total return an investor could actually have earned, including fees and distributions, so it compares fairly with holding Bitcoin. Front-month futures jump when a contract rolls, which distorts multi-year returns, but they are the cleanest daily price for a ratio like BTC/gold.

Is this investment advice?

No. The page shows public market data for information only. Past returns do not guarantee future results, and Bitcoin, metals and commodities can all fall in value.

Methodology and sources.

Returns: total return from adjusted closing prices (Yahoo Finance), in USD. The EUR view converts every value with the EUR/USD rate on each date, i.e. what a euro-based investor would have earned holding the asset unhedged. Year to date starts at the last trading day of the previous year; 1, 5 and 10-year returns start on the same calendar day of the earlier year (or the previous trading day).

Assets: Bitcoin (BTC-USD), gold (GLD), silver (SLV), WTI crude oil (USO), copper (CPER), platinum (PPLT) and a broad commodities basket (DBC, 14 commodities). Oil, copper and the basket are futures-based ETFs, so their returns include the cost of rolling contracts. Brent and natural gas are not included.

The dataset is rebuilt daily by a public script and published as JSON; the file is updated only when every check passes. The data is delayed and for information only; not investment advice.
