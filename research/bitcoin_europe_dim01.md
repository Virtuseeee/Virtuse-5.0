# Dimension 1: TAM — Total Addressable Market for Bitcoin-Only Services in Europe

**Research Date**: May 29, 2026
**Geographic Scope**: EU + UK + EFTA (European Economic Area + Switzerland)
**Definition**: Bitcoin-only services are products and services that exclusively support Bitcoin (not multi-crypto), including non-custodial/self-custody solutions, P2P platforms, Lightning Network services, payment processing, mining hosting, education, consulting, and other decentralized/non-custodial infrastructure. This analysis explicitly excludes MiCA-regulated CASPs (centralized exchanges and custodial services requiring MiCA licenses).

---

## Executive Summary

The Total Addressable Market (TAM) for Bitcoin-only services in Europe is estimated at **$8.7–11.3 billion (EUR 8.0–10.4 billion)** as of 2025, encompassing all potential demand across hardware wallets, software wallets, Lightning services, payment processing, mining hosting, education, consulting, and ancillary services. This represents a subset of the broader European cryptocurrency market valued at approximately USD 7.97 billion (2025) [^7^], with Bitcoin commanding a 38% share of the European crypto market by asset type.

The European Bitcoin-only services market is characterized by:
- **50+ million crypto users** in the EU alone (2024), with Bitcoin as the dominant asset [^26^]
- **140 million active wallet users** in Europe (2025), with ~59% preferring non-custodial/self-custody solutions [^158^]
- Strong infrastructure concentration: Europe hosts **~36.7% of all Bitcoin nodes** globally [^159^]
- Germany as the dominant hub: **14.85% of global Bitcoin nodes**, **13.4% of Lightning nodes** [^157^][^12^]
- Hardware wallet market presence with European leaders Ledger (France), Trezor (Czech Republic), and BitBox (Switzerland)

## 6. Key Limitations and Caveats

1. **Data Fragmentation**: Bitcoin-only service categories overlap (e.g., wallets with custody, Lightning with payments). Care was taken to avoid double-counting, but some overlap may remain.
2. **P2P Market Opacity**: P2P trading volume on decentralized platforms (Bisq, RoboSats) is inherently difficult to measure, leading to low-confidence estimates.
3. **MiCA Boundary**: The explicit exclusion of MiCA-regulated CASPs creates an artificial boundary. Some Bitcoin-only services (e.g., certain payment processors) may seek MiCA licensing, changing their categorization.
4. **Methodological Variations**: Market research reports use varying methodologies and definitions, leading to wide ranges in estimates. Where multiple sources conflict, ranges are provided.
5. **Currency Conversion**: EUR/USD exchange rate assumed at ~1.09. Actual rates vary.
6. **Definitional Ambiguity**: "Bitcoin-only" is not always clearly defined. Some services (e.g., Ledger) support multiple currencies but derive majority usage from Bitcoin storage.
