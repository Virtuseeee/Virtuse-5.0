# Dimension 11: Investment Flows & Capital Deployment in European Bitcoin-Only Services

**Research Date:** May 29, 2026
**Focus:** Bitcoin-only services in Europe (EU + EFTA + UK)
**Exclusions:** MiCA-regulated CASPs (centralized exchanges requiring MiCA licenses)
**Inclusions:** Non-custodial, self-custody, P2P, open-source, and decentralized Bitcoin services

---

## Executive Summary

The European Bitcoin-only services ecosystem has experienced significant capital deployment across venture funding, grant programs, and strategic investments from 2023 to 2026. Key findings include:

- **Total tracked VC funding:** Over $700 million raised by European Bitcoin-only companies since 2020
- **Grant funding:** OpenSats alone deploys ~$1 million monthly across 300+ grants
- **Bitcoin-only VC funds:** Ego Death Capital closed a $100 million Fund II in July 2025, the largest Bitcoin-only venture fund in history
- **Hardware wallet market:** Europe accounts for ~30% of global hardware wallet market share (~$2.4B in 2023)
- **Most active segments:** Hardware wallets, Bitcoin savings/DCA apps, Lightning infrastructure, and open-source development

## 5. Most Profitable Business Models in Bitcoin-Only Services

### 5.1 Hardware Wallets (Highest Revenue)
- **Model:** One-time device sale + recurring software/app ecosystem
- **Example:** Ledger at $70.9M revenue (2024), 7M+ devices sold
- **Margins:** Hardware typically 30-50% gross margin
- **Advantage:** High barriers to entry, regulatory moats, brand loyalty

### 5.2 DCA/Automated Savings Apps (Fastest Growth)
- **Model:** Transaction fees + spread on Bitcoin purchases + subscription
- **Example:** Bitstack (10x revenue growth in 2 years), Relai ($3.1M revenue, 3x growth target)
- **Key feature:** Round-up/spare change investing creates habitual usage
- **Advantage:** High retention rates (85%+), predictable recurring revenue

### 5.3 Transaction Fee Model (Most Common)
- **Model:** Percentage fee on Bitcoin buy/sell transactions
- **Examples:** Relai (1% fees), 21bitcoin (0.79% starting), Bitstack
- **Unit economics:** $18-26 ARPU, $120 CLV, 85% retention
- **Advantage:** Scales with Bitcoin price appreciation

### 5.4 Open-Source Grant-Funded Model
- **Model:** No direct revenue; funded by donations and grants
- **Example:** BTCPay Server, Bitcoin Core contributors via Brink/OpenSats
- **Sustainability:** Depends on continued philanthropic support from industry

### 5.5 B2B Infrastructure/API Model
- **Model:** Charging businesses for API access, white-label solutions
- **Example:** Relai planning B2B2C API for 2026; Voltage Lightning infrastructure
- **Advantage:** Higher contract values, recurring revenue

### 5.6 Bitcoin Treasury/Asset Management
- **Model:** Management fees on Bitcoin treasury operations
- **Example:** FUTURE Holdings AG (Switzerland), Samara Asset Group (Malta)
- **Advantage:** Institutional-grade fees, growing corporate adoption

### 6.3 Key Investor Thesis Points

1. **"Bitcoin-only" conviction:** Bitcoiners believe Bitcoin has unique properties (decentralization, Lindy effect, network security) that altcoins cannot replicate
2. **Long-term thinking:** Bitcoin-only investors typically have longer time horizons aligned with Bitcoin's 4-year cycle
3. **Protocol alignment:** Portfolio company success is tied to Bitcoin's success, creating aligned incentives
4. **Avoiding token risk:** Bitcoin-only avoids the regulatory and technical risks of altcoin/token projects
5. **Real-world problem solving:** Focus on practical applications (savings, payments, self-custody) rather than speculation

## 8. Key Trends and Outlook

### 8.1 Converging Trends
1. **MiCA compliance becoming table stakes:** Companies securing MiCA licenses (Bitstack, Relai) to unlock EU-wide marketing and operations
2. **Hardware wallet market consolidation:** Ledger preparing $4B+ IPO; Trezor remaining bootstrapped but competitive
3. **Bitcoin-only apps outperforming multi-crypto:** DCA-focused apps (Bitstack, Relai, 21bitcoin) showing strong growth
4. **Institutional Bitcoin treasury products emerging:** FUTURE Holdings in Switzerland targeting institutional adoption
5. **Open-source funding maturing:** OpenSats, Brink, HRF creating sustainable funding pipelines

### 8.2 Challenges
1. **Early-stage funding contraction:** Seed rounds down 41.8% in 2025 globally [^683^]
2. **Bitcoin Ecosystem VC declining:** Down 54.3% YoY in 2025 as investors favor general crypto infrastructure [^683^]
3. **Regulatory complexity:** MiCA compliance costs significant resources for startups
4. **Competition from multi-crypto platforms:** Revolut, Bitpanda offering Bitcoin alongside other assets

### 8.3 Outlook for 2026-2027
- Ledger IPO could unlock $4B+ valuation and validate the European Bitcoin market
- Bitcoin-only DCA apps expected to reach 1M+ users each
- Lightning Network infrastructure (Voltage, ACINQ) gaining institutional adoption
- Institutional Bitcoin treasury services expanding (FUTURE, Samara)
- Hardware wallet market projected to grow at 21%+ CAGR through 2035
