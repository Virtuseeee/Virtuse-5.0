Pick your country to see how a Bitcoin sale is taxed, with a link to the official source. Then check how ready your Bitcoin is if something happens to you.

How it works

Step 01

Gain tax, holding-period exemption and how to file, each with its official source and the date we checked it.

Six short questions show whether your heirs could reach your Bitcoin. The score is out of 100.

Questions and answers

Where do the tax rules come from?

From the tax authority or the law of each country, linked next to every rule with the date we checked it. As of Q3 2026.

Is this tax advice?

No. It is an overview to help you ask the right questions. Rules change; confirm your case with a local tax advisor.

Does Virtuse see my answers?

No. The inheritance check runs in your browser and nothing you answer is sent to Virtuse.

Which countries are covered?

13 EU countries. A country is shown only when its rules have an official source.
