Bitcoin Privacy. Within the law.

Privacy protects you from theft, profiling and targeted scams. These tools and guides help you keep your Bitcoin activity and your everyday browsing private, within the law.

9 picks · last reviewed 22 October 2026

Our picks. Each with a one-line verdict.

This page is about lawful privacy: keeping your personal and financial information safe from criminals, data brokers and needless exposure. It does not help anyone evade anti-money-laundering rules, sanctions or tax obligations. See our AML & compliance page. Looking for how Virtuse handles your data? That is our privacy policy.

Node · Open source

Bitcoin Core

Running your own node means your wallet no longer reveals your addresses to someone else's server.

bitcoincore.orgWebsite
Network privacy · Open source

Tor Project

Hides your IP address when browsing or when your node talks to the network. Free and widely audited.

torproject.orgWebsite
Operating system · Open source

Tails

A portable operating system that runs from a USB stick and leaves no trace on the computer.

tails.netWebsite
VPN · Sweden · Accepts bitcoin

Mullvad VPN

A Swedish VPN that needs no email to sign up and accepts bitcoin. Simple, honest pricing.

mullvad.netWebsite
Email · Switzerland · Accepts bitcoin

Proton

Encrypted email and storage from Switzerland. Paid plans can be settled in bitcoin.

proton.meWebsite
Standard · On-chain

Silent Payments

A reusable payment code that avoids address reuse when people pay you. Overview of the standard and supporting tools.

silentpayments.xyzWebsite
Standard · On-chain

Payjoin

A payment technique in which sender and receiver both contribute inputs, breaking common chain-analysis assumptions.

payjoin.orgWebsite
