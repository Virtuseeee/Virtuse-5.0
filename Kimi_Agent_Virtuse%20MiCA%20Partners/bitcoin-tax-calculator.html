    How the calculator works

    Step 01

    The gain

    Sale amount minus purchase cost. A loss pays no tax.

    Step 02

    Exemptions first

    Holding periods (Czechia 3 years, Croatia 2, Germany 1, Austria for coins bought before March 2021) and small-sale limits (Czechia, France, Germany) are checked before any rate.

    Step 03

    Rate or bands

    One rate, or progressive bands on your whole tax base (Slovakia, Czechia, Germany, Spain): the tax is what the gain adds to the tax on your other income.

    Common questions

    How is tax on a Bitcoin sale calculated?

    Take the sale amount, subtract what you paid (fees included), and apply your country's rate to the gain. Some countries tax the gain at one rate (Poland 19%, Austria 27.5%, France 31.4%), others add it to your other income and tax it in bands (Slovakia, Czechia, Germany, Spain).

    In which countries is a Bitcoin sale tax-free?

    Slovenia does not tax private gains. Czechia exempts coins held more than 3 years, Croatia more than 2, Germany more than 1. In Austria, coins bought before March 2021 and held over a year are tax-free. The Netherlands taxes your wealth on 1 January, not the sale.

    Is swapping Bitcoin for another crypto taxed?

    It depends on the country. Germany, Czechia and Spain tax a swap like a sale; Poland, France, Austria and Hungary do not. This calculator handles a sale for euros.

    What does the calculator leave out?

    Your other sales in the year (annual limits are applied to this sale alone), losses from earlier years, church tax and solidarity surcharge in Germany, and each country's own details listed under the result. It is one sale by a private person in tax year 2026.

    Is this tax advice?

    No. It shows the arithmetic of the published rule and its source. Before you file, check your case with a tax adviser in your country.
