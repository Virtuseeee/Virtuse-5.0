Your exchange is reporting your Bitcoin trades: DAC8 in France

Since 1 January 2026, crypto exchanges record the identity, tax ID and trades of French residents under DAC8. French providers report 2026 data to the DGFiP before 15 June 2027, around the deadline for your return. Gains on sales for euros are taxed at a 31.4% flat rate in 2026; crypto-to-crypto swaps are not taxed.

What DAC8 is

DAC8 (EU Directive 2023/2226) brings crypto into the automatic exchange of tax information that already covers bank accounts. France implemented it on time: the 2025 Finance Act added articles 1649 AC bis to 1649 AC sexies to the General Tax Code, and Decree 2025-1276 of 19 December 2025 sets the reporting rules. They apply to transactions from 1 January 2026.

Exchanges licensed in other EU countries report to their own tax authority, which sends the data to France. Providers reporting in France send it to the DGFiP (General Directorate of Public Finances).

TransfersTransfers out of the exchange, including to your own wallet

Moving Bitcoin between your own wallets is not taxed, but a transfer out of an exchange shows up in the report. Keep a record that the address is yours.

Crypto-to-crypto swaps appear in the report even though France does not tax them.

How Bitcoin is taxed in France

  • Gains on selling crypto for euros, or using it to pay for goods and services, are taxed under article 150 VH bis of the General Tax Code.
  • For 2026 gains the flat tax (PFU) is 31.4%: 12.8% income tax plus 18.6% social charges, after the 2026 social security budget raised the CSG. You can opt for the progressive income tax scale instead; social charges still apply.
  • Swapping one crypto-asset for another, stablecoins included, is not taxed. Tax arises when you convert to euros, goods or services.
  • If your total disposals in the year are €305 or less, the gains are exempt. The limit is on the sale amounts, not on the gains.
  • There is no holding-period exemption.
  • Gains are calculated on form 2086. Every crypto account held abroad, including on exchanges, must be listed on form 3916-bis, even if it was closed during the year.
  • Since the 2025 Finance Act, the tax office can go back 10 years for undeclared foreign crypto accounts.

What to do now

  1. Export your full trade history from every exchange you have used, including closed accounts.
  2. List every foreign exchange account on form 3916-bis; a missing account is fined per account.
  3. Calculate your gains on form 2086 and budget for the 31.4% rate on 2026 gains.
  4. If you left out gains or accounts in earlier years, talk to a tax advisor about a corrected return (déclaration rectificative).
  5. When you withdraw to your own wallet, keep proof that the address belongs to you.

FAQ

Do I pay tax when I swap Bitcoin for another crypto-asset?

No. In France only a conversion to euros, goods or services is taxed. The swap still appears in the DAC8 report.

Is the flat tax still 30%?

Not for 2026 gains. The CSG increase raised social charges to 18.6%, so the flat tax is 31.4%.

Is my hardware wallet reported?

No. Your wallet's balance is not reported, but transfers from an exchange to it are.

Does it help to hold Bitcoin longer?

No. France has no holding-period exemption for crypto gains.
