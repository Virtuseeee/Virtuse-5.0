Bitcoin tax in Slovenia

Bitcoin tax in Slovenia as of Q3 2026: Progressive, up to about 50% on disposal gains. Exemption: No general holding-period exemption. Filing: Annual personal income tax return. Cryptocurrency gains are treated as income; check current guidance with a local advisor. Indicative 2026 overview, not tax advice.
