Your exchange is reporting your Bitcoin trades: DAC8 in Croatia

Since 1 January 2026, crypto exchanges record the identity, tax ID and trades of Croatian residents under DAC8. Croatian providers report 2026 data to the Tax Administration by 30 June 2027. Gains are taxed at 12%, but Bitcoin sold more than two years after purchase is tax-free. Gains are reported on the JOPPD form.

What DAC8 is

DAC8 (EU Directive 2023/2226) brings crypto into the automatic exchange of tax information that already covers bank accounts. Croatia implemented it on time by amending the Act on Administrative Cooperation in Taxation (Official Gazette 146/2025). The first reporting period is 2026.

Providers reporting in Croatia send the data to the Tax Administration (Porezna uprava) of the Ministry of Finance. Exchanges licensed in other EU countries report to their own tax authority, which sends the data to Croatia.

What your exchange reports

Moving Bitcoin between your own wallets is not taxed, but a transfer out of an exchange shows up in the report. Keep a record that the address is yours.

How Bitcoin is taxed in Croatia

  • Gains from selling crypto are capital income, taxed at a final 12% since 2024. There is no local surtax on top.
  • Crypto sold more than two years after purchase is tax-free.
  • The gain is the sale price minus the purchase price and trading costs. Losses offset gains only within the same year.
  • Capital gains are not part of the annual tax return. You calculate and pay the tax yourself and file the JOPPD form by the end of February. The Tax Administration also expects a JOPPD for exempt gains.
  • The Tax Administration's published opinion treats crypto-to-crypto swaps as not taxed; tax arises when you sell for money. That opinion dates from 2018, so check it with an advisor.
  • Mining income is taxed as other income when received.

What to do now

  1. Export your full trade history from every exchange you have used, including closed accounts.
  2. Note the purchase date of every coin: the two-year exemption depends on it.
  3. File JOPPD and pay the tax on 2026 gains by 1 March 2027, including for exempt sales.
  4. If you did not report gains in earlier years, talk to a tax advisor about correcting them; the assessment period is six years.
  5. When you withdraw to your own wallet, keep proof that the address belongs to you.

FAQ

Is Bitcoin tax-free after two years in Croatia?

Yes. Crypto sold more than two years after purchase is not taxed, though a JOPPD form is still expected.

Do I report crypto gains in my annual tax return?

No. Capital gains are self-assessed on the JOPPD form by the end of February.

Is my hardware wallet reported?

No. Your wallet's balance is not reported, but transfers from an exchange to it are.

How far back can the Tax Administration check?

Generally six years, counted from 1 January of the year after the tax should have been assessed.
