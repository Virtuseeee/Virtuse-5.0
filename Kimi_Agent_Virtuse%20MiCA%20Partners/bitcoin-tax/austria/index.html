Bitcoin tax in Austria

Bitcoin tax in Austria as of Q3 2026: 27.5 % KESt (capital gains tax). Exemption: Gains tax-free after 1-year holding (private investors). Filing: Annual tax return. Staking and lending rewards are taxed when received, not when sold. Indicative 2026 overview, not tax advice.
