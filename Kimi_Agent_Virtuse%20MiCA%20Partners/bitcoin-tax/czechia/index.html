Bitcoin tax in Czechia

Updated 13 Oct 2026

Bitcoin tax in Czechia as of Q3 2026: 15% income tax (23% above threshold). Exemption: Tax-free after 3 years of holding (up to CZK 40 million a year), or if total crypto sale proceeds in the year are at most CZK 100,000. Filing: Annual personal tax return.
