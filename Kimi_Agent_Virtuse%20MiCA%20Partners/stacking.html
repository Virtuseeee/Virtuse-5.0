Stacking Strategist

Plan your Bitcoin stacking, see what it costs.

Set an amount, a rhythm and a time frame. You see a scenario projection and what the fee at each partner costs you every year.

How it works

Step 01

Set your plan

A lump sum, a weekly or monthly amount and how many years you want to buy.

Step 02

See a scenario

Three return scenarios show a range, not a forecast. Bitcoin can also lose value.

Step 03

Compare the fees

What the listed fee at each partner costs you a year at your amount. You choose where to buy.

Questions and answers

Is this a forecast?

No. The returns are scenarios to show a range. Past performance doesn't guarantee future results, and Bitcoin can fall in value.

Are spreads included?

No. The comparison uses each partner's listed fee. The Fee Index adds the estimated spread for total cost.

Why can I enter up to €1,000,000?

Some private clients and companies plan larger allocations. For those, Virtuse CFO helps plan, track and report a Bitcoin treasury.

Does Virtuse hold my Bitcoin?

No. You buy directly at the partner you choose. Virtuse never holds your keys or funds.
