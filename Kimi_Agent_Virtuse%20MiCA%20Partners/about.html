Our Story
2018

European Roots, Singapore Reach

Founded as a digital asset and commodity platform with roots in European energy markets, Virtuse expanded to Singapore to bridge traditional finance with crypto and tokenized real-world assets.

2022

The Bitcoin-Only Transition

A strategic transition made Virtuse a dedicated, Bitcoin-only exchange — trading breadth for depth, and committing fully to the hardest money ever created.

2024

From Exchange to Hub

Virtuse shifted from operating as an exchange to curating one — connecting investors directly with vetted, MiCA-licensed partners across every Bitcoin service instead of holding assets itself.

Today

A Global Network

Virtuse now operates as a global infrastructure hub, delivering a full suite of Bitcoin services to clients in over 100 countries through a secure, institutional-grade network.

Our Name

What is Virtuse?

Our name is inspired by Virtù, a concept introduced by Niccolò Machiavelli. To him, virtù wasn't about conventional morality — it was about strength, control, and the ability to command one's own reality.

Bitcoin is the modern embodiment of virtù. It provides the ultimate form of financial power and autonomy, allowing you to secure your future without external interference.

It is power you control.
