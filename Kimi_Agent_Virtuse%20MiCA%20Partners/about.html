Our Name

What is Virtuse?

Our name is inspired by Virtù, a concept introduced by Niccolò Machiavelli. To him, virtù wasn't about conventional morality — it was about strength, control, and the ability to command one's own reality.

Bitcoin is the modern embodiment of virtù. It provides the ultimate form of financial power and autonomy, allowing you to secure your future without external interference.

It is power you control.
