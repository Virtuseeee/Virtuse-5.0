ABOUT US

About Virtuse

Virtuse is the Bitcoin infrastructure hub providing the foundation for the borderless monetary economy. Founded in 2018, the company was born from the unwavering conviction that Bitcoin has the power to democratize access to the global financial system. Virtuse pioneered the space by becoming the first tokenization platform, before executing a strategic transition in 2022 to become a dedicated, Bitcoin-only exchange.

Today, the company delivers a comprehensive suite of services transforming how generational wealth is managed, built, and protected. Operating as a global Bitcoin-only infrastructure hub, Virtuse serves clients in over 100 countries within a secure, institutional-grade environment.

This infrastructure is underpinned by Virtuse Wealth Management a.s., a proud member of the multinational Virtuse Group with a strategic presence spanning the EU and Asia. The company anchors its global operations from its headquarters in the technological hub of Singapore, while maintaining deep roots and local expertise through its operational office in Bratislava.

The company's mission is to help investors not only build but effectively protect their generational wealth through an uncompromising commitment to Bitcoin, financial sovereignty, and secure monetary freedom. Operating through a robust network of over 50 strategic partners worldwide, Virtuse ensures maximum security and institutional liquidity for its global clientele, serving as a trusted bridge to the Bitcoin economy.

OUR NAME

What is Virtuse?

Our name is inspired by Virtù, a concept introduced by Niccolò Machiavelli. To him, virtù wasn't about conventional morality — it was about strength, control, and the ability to command one's own reality.

Bitcoin is the modern embodiment of virtù. It provides the ultimate form of financial power and autonomy, allowing you to secure your future without external interference. It is power you control.
