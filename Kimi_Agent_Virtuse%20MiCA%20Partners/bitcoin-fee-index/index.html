How we calculate it

Step 01

Listed fee

The fee each partner publishes for this route at the entry tier, from the partner's own fee page.

Step 02

Estimated spread

What you pay above the market's mid price when the order fills. We measure it rather than assume zero.

Step 03

Total cost

Fee plus spread, as a share of each buy. Per year means 12 buys of the chosen amount.

Questions and answers

What does the Bitcoin Fee Index rank?

EU-licensed routes for buying Bitcoin every month, ranked by total cost: the listed fee plus the estimated spread, at €50 to €1,000 a month.

Why isn't a 0% fee automatically the cheapest?

A route can charge no fee and still price Bitcoin above the market. That's why we add the estimated spread to the fee before ranking.

Are these all the costs?

No. Network and withdrawal fees, and currency conversion if you don't pay in euros, are not included. They depend on how and when you move your Bitcoin.

How often are the numbers checked?

We check the fee pages and measure the spreads every quarter, and whenever a partner changes its prices. The date at the top shows the last check.
