FAQ

What does the Bitcoin Fee Index rank?

The annual fee drag of partner buy routes as of Q4 2026, calculated with the Stacking Strategist formula from listed percentage fees and monthly subscriptions.

Who is cheapest at €100 per month?

21bitcoin: a 0% variable fee and €0 in annual fee drag at that amount.

Why is RevenueBot not ranked?

RevenueBot charges no fee per purchase. It takes 20% of the bot’s profit, capped at $50 a month, so it cannot be ranked on purchase cost.
