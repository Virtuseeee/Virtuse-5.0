Livres sur le Bitcoin pour vos prochaines lectures.

Les livres que nous recommandons le plus souvent, de l'histoire monétaire au fonctionnement du protocole. Chacun est accompagné d'un avis d'une ligne sur le public visé, pour vous aider à choisir votre prochaine lecture.

8 sélections · dernière vérification le 22 octobre 2026

The Bitcoin Standard (Saifedean Ammous) The classic case for Bitcoin as sound money, told through the history of money. Opinionated and influential.saifedean.com · Economics · Beginner Broken Money (Lyn Alden) A calm, well-researched history of money and banking that ends with Bitcoin. The best pick for sceptical readers.lynalden.com · Economics · History The Fiat Standard (Saifedean Ammous) The companion to The Bitcoin Standard: how the current money system works and what it costs.saifedean.com · Economics Layered Money (Nik Bhatia) A short, clear explanation of how money is built in layers, from gold to central banks to Bitcoin and Lightning.layeredmoney.com · Economics · Short read The Price of Tomorrow (Jeff Booth) Why technology pushes prices down while the monetary system fights it. Not a Bitcoin book first, but a strong argument for one.thepriceoftomorrow.com · Economics · Technology The Bullish Case for Bitcoin (Vijay Boyapati) The famous essay expanded into a book: why Bitcoin could become a global store of value, step by step.bullishcaseforbitcoin.com · Investment thesis Mastering Bitcoin (Antonopoulos, Harding) The standard technical reference for developers and curious engineers. Free to read on GitHub.github.com · Technical · Free · Open source Grokking Bitcoin (Kalle Rosenbaum) A gentle, illustrated walk through how Bitcoin works, written by a Swedish developer. Free on GitHub.github.com · Technical · Free · European author
