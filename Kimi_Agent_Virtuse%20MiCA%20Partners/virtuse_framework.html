1. The Critical Legal Boundary

MiCA defines ten regulated crypto-asset services (Article 3(16)) that require a CASP license. Virtuse must never cross into these activities. The table below maps each regulated service against Virtuse's intended operations.

The Three Red Lines

1. No custody -- Virtuse never holds private keys or funds.

2. No execution -- Virtuse never signs transactions or places orders.

3. No personalised advice -- All content is uniform, never tailored to individual circumstances.

2. Five Compliant Business Lines

2.1 Bitcoin Information & Media Platform

Legal Basis

General market information is NOT "advice" under MiCA. MiCA Article 3(16)(10) defines "advice" as personalised recommendations to a client in respect of one or more transactions relating to crypto-assets, where the recommendation is based on an assessment of the client's personal circumstances. Uniform content delivered to all subscribers falls outside this definition.

Virtuse's existing Terms of Service already state: "All opinions, information, discussions, analyses, prices, advice and other information on this Website are general market reviews and do not constitute any investment advice." This positioning is legally sound under MiCA.

What MiCA Defines as "Advice"What Virtuse CAN Do
Personalised recommendations considering client's circumstances, objectives, risk appetiteGeneral market commentary, price data, charts, Bitcoin news
Tailored buy/sell recommendationsUniform research reports, market newsletters, on-chain analytics
Individual portfolio suggestionsBitcoin education courses, video content, podcasts

Key Safeguards

  • No questionnaires profiling users (income, net worth, risk tolerance)
  • No "based on your profile, we recommend..." functionality
  • All content identical for all subscribers at the same tier
  • Clear disclaimers on every page: "For informational purposes only; not investment advice"

2.2 Referral & Affiliate Partnerships with Licensed CASPs

Legal Basis

MiCA applies to the CASP being promoted, not necessarily the referrer -- provided the referrer does not execute, custody, or advise. Pure referral links where the user completes onboarding independently on the licensed CASP's platform fall outside MiCA's scope.

2.3 Non-Custodial Trading Bot Infrastructure

Legal Basis

Non-custodial bot software is a tool, not a financial intermediary. ESMA guidance confirms that "fully decentralised manner without any intermediary" falls outside MiCA scope. The key determinant is who controls the private keys.

Architecture Options

ArchitectureMiCA StatusHow It Works
Self-hosted bot NOT a CASP User downloads/runs bot locally; connects own wallet; Virtuse provides strategy templates only
Cloud bot with user-controlled API keys NOT a CASP Bot runs on Virtuse cloud BUT user retains sole control via API key permissions (no withdrawals); non-custodial
Smart contract DCA (on-chain) NOT a CASP User signs transactions themselves; smart contract executes pre-programmed DCA strategy
Critical Technical Requirement

Virtuse must never have the ability to initiate withdrawals, sign transactions on user's behalf, or access private keys. The user must authorise every action with their own signature.

2.4 Bitcoin Wallet Integration (Non-Custodial)

Legal Basis

MiCA Recital 83 explicitly excludes "hardware or software providers of non-custodial wallets" from its scope. Non-custodial wallet software is unregulated in the EU.

2.5 HNWI Media & Research Services

Legal Basis

Information sold as a product is NOT "advice" if non-personalised and not recommendation-based. MiCA applies to the activity of advising, not the sale of research publications. The critical distinction is whether content is tailored to an individual's circumstances.

The Critical Line

These services must be genuinely educational and informational. The moment Virtuse says "based on your situation, you should buy X amount of Bitcoin," it crosses into regulated "advice" territory.

Safeguards for HNWI Service

  • Clear disclaimers: "For informational purposes only; not investment advice"
  • No client profiling (income, risk tolerance, investment goals)
  • No individualised portfolio recommendations
  • Content is the same for all subscribers at a given tier
  • Group sessions only (no one-on-one "advisory" calls)

Recommended Legal Protections

1. Obtain a formal legal opinion from a Slovak law firm specialising in financial regulation confirming Virtuse's activities fall outside CASP scope.

2. Register with Narodna banka Slovenska (NBS) as an information service provider (voluntary notification, not licensing).

3. Maintain annual compliance reviews to ensure no feature drift into regulated territory.

4. Secure professional indemnity insurance covering media/content liability (EUR 1-2M coverage recommended).
