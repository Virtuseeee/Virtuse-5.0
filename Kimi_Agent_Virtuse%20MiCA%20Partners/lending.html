P2P Marketplace Model

💰 Borrowers

Use your Bitcoin as collateral to borrow fiat (EUR, CHF, CZK) or stablecoins (USDC). Keep your Bitcoin exposure, avoid capital gains taxes, and access liquidity without selling. LTV up to 50%. Terms from 3 to 24 months. Rates from 5% p.a.
