Tax in the Netherlands

Gain tax: Box 3 wealth tax on a deemed return (about 36% of the assumed yield). Exemption: Tax-free allowance (€59,357 per person, 2026).

FAQ

How do bitcoin buy fees compare in the Netherlands as of Q4 2026?

Lowest published fee at €100/month: 21bitcoin (Auto-Invest plan), a 0% variable fee and €0 a year. That ranking uses the published partner fee schedule, not spreads or currency conversion. Lowest total cost incl. spread on the Virtuse Bitcoin Fee Index: Bybit EU (0.25%); 21bitcoin is not ranked there until its spread is measured.

Does buying bitcoin trigger tax in the Netherlands?

The Netherlands does not use a classic capital gains tax. Box 3 wealth tax on a deemed return still applies to holdings (as of Q4 2026: Box 3 wealth tax on a deemed return (about 36% of the assumed yield)).

Are these fees the full cost of buying?

No. This ranking uses the published percentage fee plus any listed monthly subscription from the Stacking Strategist fee schedule. Spreads and network (miner) fees are not included. The Bitcoin Fee Index adds the estimated spread.

Does Virtuse execute the buy?

No. Virtuse never holds your keys. You complete KYC on the partner platform and buy there.
