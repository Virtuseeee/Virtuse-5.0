#

21 million cap

#
Basics

The protocol's hard limit on the bitcoin supply. New coins enter only through the block reward, which halves every 210,000 blocks.

  • Also called: supply cap, max supply
  • Related on Virtuse: Halving, Bitcoin Data
  • See also: Halving
  • Source: Bitcoin Core: nSubsidyHalvingInterval = 210000

200-week moving average

#
Markets

Bitcoin's average price over the last 200 weeks, a long-term trend line.

  • Also called: 200 WMA, moving average
  • Related on Virtuse: 200-week MA chart
  • See also: Drawdown

A

AMLR and AMLA

#
EU rules

The EU anti-money-laundering regulation (EU) 2024/1624 applies from 10 July 2027. From then, credit institutions, financial institutions and CASPs may not keep anonymous crypto-asset accounts or accounts that anonymise transactions, including through anonymity-enhancing coins. This concerns accounts at providers, not coins in your own wallet. AMLA, the new EU anti-money-laundering authority, has its seat in Frankfurt am Main.

  • Also called: anti-money laundering, AML, privacy coins
  • Related on Virtuse: AML & compliance
  • See also: KYC, Travel Rule
  • Source: Regulation (EU) 2024/1624, Articles 79 and 90; Regulation (EU) 2024/1620, Article 4

Assisted custody

#
Custody & security

Multisig in which you hold most keys and a provider holds one to help with recovery and heirs. The provider cannot move the coins alone.

  • Also called: collaborative custody
  • Related on Virtuse: Custody, Partner Finder
  • See also: Multisig, Bitcoin inheritance plan

B

Bitcoin (BTC)

#
Basics

Digital money with no central issuer, run by a public network since its first block on 3 January 2009. The protocol caps the supply at 21 million coins.

  • Also called: BTC
  • Related on Virtuse: What is Bitcoin?
  • See also: Satoshi (sat), 21 million cap
  • Source: Bitcoin white paper; Bitcoin Core: MAX_MONEY = 21,000,000 BTC

Bitcoin dominance

#
Markets

Bitcoin's share of the total market value of all crypto-assets.

  • Also called: dominance
  • Related on Virtuse: Dominance chart
  • See also: Bitcoin-only

Bitcoin ETP

#
Markets

An exchange-traded product that tracks the price of bitcoin and trades on a stock exchange. You hold a security, not bitcoin or keys.

  • Also called: ETN, exchange-traded product
  • Related on Virtuse: Buy Bitcoin
  • See also: Spot Bitcoin ETF, Self-custody

Bitcoin inheritance plan

#
Custody & security

What your heirs need to find, reach and move your bitcoin, without exposing the keys while you are alive.

  • Also called: inheritance, heirs
  • Related on Virtuse: Inheritance checklist, Tax & Inheritance Agent
  • See also: Multisig, Seed phrase

Bitcoin tax in Czechia

#
Tax

A gain is tax-free once you have held the crypto-asset for more than 3 years (capped at CZK 40 million a year together with some other exempt income), or if your total crypto-asset sale proceeds in the year are at most CZK 100,000. Neither applies to business assets.

  • Also called: Czechia, Czech Republic, Česko
  • Related on Virtuse: Czechia tax guide, Tax Agent: Czechia
  • See also: Holding-period exemption
  • Source: Finanční správa: Osvobození příjmů z úplatného převodu kryptoaktiv (KV 29. 4. 2026)

Bitcoin tax in Slovakia

#
Tax

Income from selling a crypto-asset is taxable "other income" under section 8(1)(t) of the Income Tax Act, and if your costs are higher than the income, the difference is not taken into account. Rates and the tax return: see the Slovakia guide.

  • Also called: Slovakia, Slovensko
  • Related on Virtuse: Slovakia tax guide, Tax Agent: Slovakia
  • See also: Taxable event, DAC8
  • Source: Finančná správa: Príjmy z predaja kryptoaktív u nepodnikateľa

Bitcoin treasury company

#
Markets

A listed company that holds bitcoin on its balance sheet as a reserve asset.

  • Also called: corporate treasury
  • Related on Virtuse: Treasury table, Treasury
  • See also: Custodian
  • Source: CoinGecko public treasuries

Bitcoin-backed loan (LTV)

#
Markets

Borrowing against bitcoin instead of selling it. LTV (loan-to-value) is the loan as a share of the collateral; if the price falls far enough, the collateral can be sold off (liquidation).

  • Also called: loan-to-value, LTV, collateral, liquidation
  • Related on Virtuse: Loan Copilot, Sell vs borrow
  • See also: Drawdown

Bitcoin-only

#
Basics

Covering Bitcoin and nothing else: no altcoins, no tokens. Virtuse has been Bitcoin-only since 2022.

  • Related on Virtuse: About Virtuse
  • See also: Non-custodial

Blockchain

#
Basics

Bitcoin's public ledger: a chain of blocks in which every block points to the one before it. A new block arrives about every 10 minutes on average.

  • Also called: ledger
  • Related on Virtuse: Bitcoin Data
  • See also: Proof of work, Node
  • Source: Bitcoin white paper

C

CASP

#
EU rules

Crypto-asset service provider: a firm authorised under MiCA by a national authority. One authorisation lets it serve the whole EU (passporting). You can check a firm in the ESMA register.

  • Also called: crypto-asset service provider, licence, passporting
  • Related on Virtuse: Partner Finder
  • See also: MiCA, KYC
  • Source: Regulation (EU) 2023/1114; ESMA: MiCA and the interim register

Cold storage

#
Custody & security

Keys that never touch a device connected to the internet.

  • Also called: offline storage
  • Related on Virtuse: Custody
  • See also: Hardware wallet

CPI

#
Macro

The US Consumer Price Index from the Bureau of Labor Statistics, the inflation figure markets watch most.

  • Also called: Consumer Price Index, US inflation
  • Related on Virtuse: Virtuse Brief
  • See also: Inflation, HICP
  • Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics: CPI

Custodian

#
Custody & security

A company that holds keys for you, such as an exchange or a regulated custodian. Convenient, but you rely on it.

  • Also called: custody provider
  • Related on Virtuse: Treasury
  • See also: Self-custody, Proof of reserves

D

DAC8

#
EU rules

EU tax directive (EU) 2023/2226. Since 1 January 2026, crypto-asset service providers record your identity, tax number and transactions and report them to the tax authorities. The first exchange between countries is due within nine months after the end of 2026, by 30 September 2027.

  • Also called: Directive 2023/2226, crypto tax reporting
  • Related on Virtuse: DAC8 in Slovakia, DAC8 in Czechia
  • See also: CASP, Taxable event
  • Source: Directive (EU) 2023/2226; European Commission: DAC8

DCA (dollar-cost averaging)

#
Markets

Buying a fixed euro amount on a schedule instead of all at once.

  • Also called: cost averaging, regular buying, stacking
  • Related on Virtuse: DCA calculator, Stacking Strategist
  • See also: Drawdown

Difficulty adjustment

#
Network & mining

Every 2,016 blocks the network resets how hard mining is, so that blocks keep arriving about every 10 minutes.

  • Also called: difficulty
  • Related on Virtuse: Mining
  • See also: Hashrate
  • Source: Bitcoin developer guide: block chain

Digital euro

#
EU rules

A proposed digital form of cash that the ECB would issue. It has not been adopted. On 9 July 2026 the European Parliament agreed to start negotiations with the Council. The ECB says that if EU lawmakers adopt the regulation in the course of 2026, the digital euro could be issued during 2029.

  • Also called: CBDC
  • Related on Virtuse: Who controls your money?
  • See also: ECB deposit facility rate
  • Source: ECB: Digital euro progress; European Parliament press release, 9 July 2026

Drawdown

#
Markets

The fall from a previous peak. On month-end closes Bitcoin fell 70% or more four times between 2011 and 2022, which is why Verdict tests a plan against a 70% fall.

  • Also called: crash, fall
  • Related on Virtuse: Verdict, Monthly returns
  • See also: DCA (dollar-cost averaging), 200-week moving average
  • Source: Virtuse data: Bitcoin month-end closes (CoinMetrics)

DXY (US dollar index)

#
Macro

The value of the dollar against six major currencies. The euro has the largest weight.

  • Also called: dollar index, USDX
  • Related on Virtuse: Virtuse Brief
  • See also: Fed funds rate
  • Source: ICE: U.S. Dollar Index

E

ECB deposit facility rate

#
Macro

The rate banks earn on overnight deposits with the Eurosystem, the rate the ECB uses to steer monetary policy.

  • Also called: ECB rate, deposit rate
  • Related on Virtuse: Virtuse Brief
  • See also: Fed funds rate, HICP
  • Source: ECB: key interest rates

F

Fear & Greed Index

#
Markets

A sentiment gauge for bitcoin from 0 to 100: 0 means extreme fear, 100 extreme greed.

  • Also called: sentiment
  • Related on Virtuse: Fear & Greed chart
  • See also: 200-week moving average
  • Source: alternative.me Fear & Greed Index

Fed funds rate

#
Macro

The US central bank's key interest rate, which the FOMC sets as a target range.

  • Also called: Federal Reserve, FOMC
  • Related on Virtuse: Virtuse Brief
  • See also: ECB deposit facility rate, US 10-year yield
  • Source: Federal Reserve: FOMC target range

H

Halving

#
Network & mining

Every 210,000 blocks, about four years, the new-coin reward per block is cut in half. The halving at block 840,000 on 20 April 2024 cut it to 3.125 BTC.

  • Also called: halvening
  • Related on Virtuse: Root-scale cycles, Monthly returns
  • See also: 21 million cap, Mining
  • Source: mempool.space: block 840,000 (20 April 2024); Bitcoin Core: nSubsidyHalvingInterval = 210000

Hardware wallet

#
Custody & security

A small device that keeps keys offline and signs transactions without showing the keys to your computer.

  • Also called: signing device
  • Related on Virtuse: Custody
  • See also: Cold storage, Self-custody

Hashrate

#
Network & mining

The total computing power securing the network.

  • Also called: hash rate
  • Related on Virtuse: Mining, Bitcoin Data
  • See also: Proof of work, Difficulty adjustment

HICP

#
Macro

The euro area's harmonised index of consumer prices, published by Eurostat. The ECB aims for 2% inflation over the medium term.

  • Also called: euro area inflation, harmonised index
  • Related on Virtuse: Virtuse Brief
  • See also: Inflation, ECB deposit facility rate
  • Source: Eurostat: HICP; ECB: monetary policy strategy

Holding-period exemption

#
Tax

Some countries stop taxing a gain once you have held the bitcoin long enough. Czechia does after more than three years; which other countries do is in the country tax guides.

  • Also called: tax-free after holding
  • Related on Virtuse: Country tax guides
  • See also: Bitcoin tax in Czechia, Taxable event
  • Source: Finanční správa: Osvobození příjmů z úplatného převodu kryptoaktiv (KV 29. 4. 2026)

I

Inflation

#
Macro

How fast prices rise, usually measured year on year. It erodes cash, and it is one reason people hold bitcoin.

  • Also called: price rises
  • Related on Virtuse: Virtuse Brief, Bitcoin vs gold
  • See also: HICP, CPI
  • Source: Eurostat: HICP

K

KYC

#
EU rules

Know your customer: the identity checks a regulated provider must carry out before it opens an account or carries out a transaction for you.

  • Also called: know your customer, identity check
  • Related on Virtuse: AML & compliance
  • See also: CASP, AMLR and AMLA
  • Source: Directive (EU) 2015/849, Articles 13 and 14

L

Lightning Network

#
Network & mining

A layer on top of Bitcoin for fast, low-fee payments through payment channels.

  • Also called: Lightning
  • Related on Virtuse: Spend Bitcoin
  • See also: Transaction fee
  • Source: Lightning Network paper

M

M2 money supply

#
Macro

Cash, deposits and other liquid money in an economy. Its growth is a common gauge of liquidity.

  • Also called: money supply, liquidity
  • Related on Virtuse: Virtuse Brief
  • See also: QE / QT, Inflation
  • Source: Federal Reserve: H.6 Money Stock Measures

Mempool

#
Network & mining

The queue of transactions waiting to get into a block. When it is full, fees rise.

  • Related on Virtuse: Bitcoin Data
  • See also: Transaction fee
  • Source: mempool.space

MiCA

#
EU rules

The EU's Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (EU) 2023/1114. Crypto-asset service providers need an EU authorisation; the national transition periods ended on 1 July 2026 at the latest.

  • Also called: Markets in Crypto-Assets, Regulation 2023/1114
  • Related on Virtuse: Buy Bitcoin, Partner Finder
  • See also: CASP, Travel Rule
  • Source: Regulation (EU) 2023/1114, Article 143(3)

Mining

#
Network & mining

Running specialised machines that do proof of work to earn new bitcoin and transaction fees.

  • Also called: miner
  • Related on Virtuse: Mining
  • See also: Proof of work, Halving

Multisig

#
Custody & security

A wallet that needs several keys to spend, for example 2 of 3, so one lost or stolen key is not the end.

  • Also called: multi-signature
  • Related on Virtuse: Custody, Inheritance
  • See also: Assisted custody

N

Node

#
Basics

Software that keeps its own copy of the blockchain and checks every rule itself, so you do not have to trust anyone else's copy.

  • Also called: full node
  • Related on Virtuse: Privacy tools
  • See also: Blockchain
  • Source: bitcoin.org: Running a full node

Non-custodial

#
Custody & security

A service that never holds your funds or keys. Virtuse is a non-custodial hub: partners provide the services under their own licences.

  • Related on Virtuse: How Virtuse earns, About Virtuse
  • See also: Custodian

P

Passphrase

#
Custody & security

An optional extra word added to a seed phrase. A different passphrase restores a different wallet, so without the right one the coins do not appear.

  • Also called: 25th word
  • Related on Virtuse: Custody
  • See also: Seed phrase
  • Source: BIP39

Private key

#
Custody & security

The secret that authorises spending. Whoever has it controls the coins.

  • Also called: key
  • Related on Virtuse: Custody
  • See also: Seed phrase

Proof of reserves

#
Custody & security

A check that a custodian holds the coins it says it holds. It only means something if the liabilities are shown too.

  • Also called: PoR
  • Related on Virtuse: Partner Finder
  • See also: Custodian

Proof of work

#
Basics

How miners compete to add the next block by spending computing power. It makes rewriting past blocks expensive.

  • Also called: PoW
  • Related on Virtuse: Mining
  • See also: Mining, Hashrate
  • Source: Bitcoin white paper

Q

QE / QT

#
Macro

Quantitative easing, when a central bank buys bonds and adds money to the system, and quantitative tightening, its reverse.

  • Also called: quantitative easing, quantitative tightening
  • Related on Virtuse: Virtuse Brief
  • See also: M2 money supply
  • Source: ECB: asset purchase programmes

R

Real yield

#
Macro

The yield on inflation-protected bonds, roughly a bond yield minus expected inflation. When real yields rise, holding assets that pay no interest, like gold or bitcoin, costs more.

  • Also called: TIPS
  • Related on Virtuse: Bitcoin vs gold
  • See also: US 10-year yield, Inflation
  • Source: U.S. Treasury: daily real yield curve rates

S

Satoshi (sat)

#
Basics

The smallest unit of bitcoin: 1 BTC = 100,000,000 sats.

  • Also called: sats, sat
  • Related on Virtuse: Bitcoin Data
  • See also: Bitcoin (BTC)
  • Source: Bitcoin Core: COIN = 100,000,000

Seed phrase

#
Custody & security

Usually 12 or 24 words from which every key in a wallet can be restored. Never type it into a website or keep it in a photo app.

  • Also called: recovery phrase, mnemonic, BIP39
  • Related on Virtuse: Custody, Inheritance
  • See also: Passphrase, Private key
  • Source: BIP39

Self-custody

#
Custody & security

You hold the keys to your bitcoin yourself. Nobody can freeze or lose them for you, and recovery is your job.

  • Also called: own keys
  • Related on Virtuse: Custody
  • See also: Private key, Hardware wallet

Spot Bitcoin ETF

#
Markets

A US-listed fund that holds bitcoin. The SEC approved the first ones on 10 January 2024. You own fund shares, not bitcoin or keys.

  • Also called: ETF
  • Related on Virtuse: Virtuse Brief
  • See also: Bitcoin ETP, Custodian
  • Source: SEC Chair statement, 10 January 2024

T

Taxable event

#
Tax

What triggers tax on bitcoin. Rules differ by country: in Slovakia both a sale and a swap into another crypto-asset are taxable, while in Poland a swap between crypto-assets is not.

  • Also called: capital gains, disposal
  • Related on Virtuse: Country tax guides, Tax & Inheritance Agent
  • See also: Holding-period exemption, Bitcoin tax in Slovakia
  • Source: Finančná správa: Príjmy z predaja kryptoaktív u nepodnikateľa; Ministerstwo Finansów: Zbycie kryptowalut

Transaction fee

#
Network & mining

What you pay miners to include a transaction, priced in sats per virtual byte (sat/vB), not by the amount sent. Partner buying fees are a different thing: see the Fee Index.

  • Also called: network fee, sat/vB
  • Related on Virtuse: Bitcoin Data, Fee Index (buying fees)
  • See also: Mempool, UTXO
  • Source: mempool.space

Travel Rule

#
EU rules

Since 30 December 2024, Regulation (EU) 2023/1113 requires sender and recipient details to travel with crypto transfers between providers. For a transfer exceeding €1,000 to or from a self-hosted wallet, the provider must take adequate measures to assess that the customer owns or controls that wallet.

  • Also called: transfer of funds, Regulation 2023/1113
  • Related on Virtuse: AML & compliance
  • See also: Self-custody, CASP
  • Source: Regulation (EU) 2023/1113, Articles 14(5), 16(2) and 40

U

US 10-year yield

#
Macro

What the US government pays to borrow for 10 years, a benchmark for the cost of money worldwide.

  • Also called: 10Y, Treasury yield
  • Related on Virtuse: Virtuse Brief
  • See also: Real yield
  • Source: U.S. Treasury: daily yield curve rates

UTXO

#
Network & mining

Unspent transaction output: the pieces of bitcoin a wallet actually holds. Many small UTXOs make later transactions more expensive.

  • Also called: unspent transaction output
  • Related on Virtuse: Bitcoin Data
  • See also: Transaction fee

V

VAT on bitcoin

#
EU rules

Exchanging euros for bitcoin and back is exempt from VAT in the EU, the EU Court of Justice ruled in the Hedqvist case (C-264/14) on 22 October 2015.

  • Also called: VAT, Hedqvist
  • Related on Virtuse: Bitcoin tax reporting
  • See also: Taxable event
  • Source: Court of Justice of the EU, C-264/14 Hedqvist, 22 October 2015
