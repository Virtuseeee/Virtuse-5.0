Questions. Read these before you file.

Do I pay tax when I buy Bitcoin?

Generally there is no tax on the purchase itself. Rules differ for selling, swapping and spending; see your country guide. This is general information, not tax advice. Country guides →

Which tool should I pick?

Start with the "Typical use" column above and check that your country is supported. Each provider lists its supported countries and current prices.

Does Virtuse see my transactions?

No. You import your data directly into the provider. Virtuse never holds coins and receives no data from these tools.
