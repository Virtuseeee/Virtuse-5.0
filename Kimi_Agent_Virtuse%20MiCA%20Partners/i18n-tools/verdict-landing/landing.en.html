Ten signals. Three questions.

Every plan is scored on the same ten signals, grouped into three questions: do you know why, does it survive, and will you stick to it. The signal map shows the whole shape in one view.

Each signal is scored 0 to 10; the score is their sum, shown rounded to 5. It measures how complete your written plan is, not whether Bitcoin is a good buy.

FAQ

Customer questions.

Will Verdict tell me whether to buy Bitcoin?

No. It rates whether your thinking is complete. What you do is your decision. Verdict doesn't recommend buying, selling, borrowing or any partner.

What does it cost?

Verdict is free. A full report costs €14, once; for now you can join the waitlist for it. Virtuse CFO is planned at €9 a month or €90 a year, with unlimited verdicts and reports.

Where does Verdict get its facts?

From the official sources Virtuse already uses, such as the tax rules of 13 countries, each with the date it was checked. When a fact is missing, Verdict asks you.

What happens to what I write?

Without an account we don't store the text of your thinking. To prevent abuse we count requests per IP address, stored only as a fingerprint (hash); the counter is deleted after 24 hours. Details in the Privacy Policy.

What can't Verdict do?

Move money: no keys, no coins, no euros; read-only, always. Place orders: buying happens on the partner's own licensed platform. Tell you to buy or sell: Verdict declines buy, sell and timing questions.

Do I need an account or a card?

No. Verdict needs neither.

Is this investment or tax advice?

No. Verdict rates how complete your thinking is. What you do with it is your decision. For tax questions, ask a tax adviser.
