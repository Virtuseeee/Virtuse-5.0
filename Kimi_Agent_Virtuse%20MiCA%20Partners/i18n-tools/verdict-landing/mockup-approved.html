FAQ

Customer questions.

Will Verdict tell me whether to buy Bitcoin?

No. It rates whether your thinking is complete. What you do is your decision. Verdict doesn't recommend buying, selling, borrowing or any partner.

What does it cost?

Verdict and the fee audit are free. A full report costs €14, once. Virtuse CFO is planned at €9 a month or €90 a year, with unlimited verdicts and reports. Join the waitlist and we'll tell you when it opens.

Where does Verdict get its facts?

From the official sources Virtuse already uses, such as the tax rules of 13 countries, each with the date it was checked. When a fact is missing, Verdict asks you.

What happens to what I write?

Without an account we don't store the text of your thinking. To prevent abuse we count requests per IP address, stored only as a fingerprint (hash); the counter is deleted after 24 hours. Details in the Privacy Policy. With a Virtuse CFO account you can keep your decision history and delete it at any time.

Do I need an account or a card?

No. The free audit needs neither. You only create an account if you later choose Virtuse CFO.

Is this investment or tax advice?

No. The audit shows what you paid and the published fees of licensed partners. What you do with it is your decision. For tax questions, ask a tax adviser.
