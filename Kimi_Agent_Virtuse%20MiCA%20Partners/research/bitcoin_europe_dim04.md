# Dimension 4: Bitcoin Hardware & Signing Devices Market in Europe

**Research Date**: May 29, 2026
**Focus**: Bitcoin-only hardware wallets, signing devices, and related physical security products in Europe (EU + EFTA + UK)
**Exclusions**: MiCA-regulated CASPs, general crypto/multi-crypto wallets (where possible)

---

## Executive Summary

The European market for Bitcoin hardware wallets and signing devices represents a significant and growing segment of the global hardware wallet industry. Europe accounts for approximately **30% of global hardware wallet demand**, with over **1.6 million units shipped across the EU in 2024 alone** [^37^]. The region is home to two of the world's largest hardware wallet manufacturers -- **Ledger (France)** and **Trezor (Czech Republic)** -- as well as the Bitcoin-only specialist **Shift Crypto/BitBox (Switzerland)**.

The global hardware wallet market was valued at **$680 million in 2025** and is projected to reach **$4.77 billion by 2035** at a 21% CAGR [^3^]. Bitcoin-only devices represent a distinct and growing niche within this market, driven by increasing awareness of security trade-offs associated with multi-crypto support, the rise of open-source hardware movements, and growing Bitcoin maximalist adoption.

## 10. Key Findings & Summary

### 10.1 European Bitcoin Hardware Wallet Market Estimates (2024-2025)

| Metric | Estimate |
|--------|----------|
| Total EU hardware wallet units (2024) | 1.6 million+ |
| Total EU hardware wallet revenue (2024) | $128M - $204M |
| Bitcoin-only share (units) | ~10-15% (160K-240K units) |
| Bitcoin-only revenue (EU est.) | ~$19M - $29M |
| European-manufactured Bitcoin-only | ~2-3% of total (primarily BitBox02) |
| CAGR (total market) | 21-26% |

### 10.2 Key Insights

1. **Europe holds 30% of global hardware wallet market** (~$204M in 2024), led by Germany, France, and Netherlands.

2. **Ledger (France) and Trezor (Czech Republic) dominate** -- both multi-crypto, combined 70%+ market share. Neither is Bitcoin-only.

3. **BitBox02 Bitcoin-Only Edition (Switzerland)** is the only significant Bitcoin-only hardware wallet both designed AND manufactured in Europe.

4. **Bitcoin-only devices represent ~10-15% of the market** by units, led by Coldcard, Passport, Jade, and BitBox02 Bitcoin-only.

5. **MiCA regulation is a tailwind** -- Article 76 asset segregation requirements and EAL6+ best practice recommendations drive demand for certified hardware.

6. **The Bitcoin-only segment is growing faster than the overall market** due to self-custody awareness, security minimalism, and open-source preference.

7. **Average selling prices range from $45 (DIY) to $400 (premium)**, with the sweet spot at $100-150 for most Bitcoin-only devices.

8. **November 2024 saw record-breaking sales** across manufacturers (Trezor: +600% weekly sales, Ledger: best revenue days ever), correlated with Bitcoin approaching $100K.

9. **Import dependence is high for Bitcoin-only devices** -- Coldcard (Canada), Passport (USA), and Jade (Canada) are all imported; only BitBox02 is European-made.

10. **Unit economics**: ~5.8M hardware wallets shipped globally in 2024, of which ~3.5M were Ledger and ~2.4M Trezor (though cumulative Trezor figures suggest the 2.4M may include channel inventory).
