# Dimension 10: Top 5 Trends Shaping Demand for Bitcoin-Only Services in Europe

**Research Date**: May 29, 2026
**Scope**: Bitcoin-only services (non-custodial, self-custody, P2P, open-source, decentralized) in Europe (EU + EFTA + UK)
**Exclusions**: MiCA-regulated CASPs (centralized exchanges, custodial services requiring MiCA licenses)

---

## Executive Summary

This research identifies five evidence-backed trends that are actively shaping demand for Bitcoin-only services in Europe as of 2026. These trends are driven by regulatory shifts (MiCA), technological maturation (Lightning Network), institutional entry (banks, pension funds, family offices), and evolving user behavior (self-custody, wealth preservation). Collectively, these trends represent a structural reconfiguration of how European individuals and institutions access, hold, and transact with Bitcoin.

---

## Trend 1: Regulatory Arbitrage Post-MiCA — The Push Toward Non-Custodial Bitcoin Infrastructure

### Description

The Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation (MiCA), fully in force since December 30, 2024, with a July 1, 2026 compliance deadline, is creating a significant demand shift toward non-custodial, Bitcoin-only services. MiCA explicitly excludes "hardware or software providers of non-custodial wallets" from its scope [^529^], creating a clear regulatory boundary that favors self-custody solutions over custodial alternatives. This regulatory arbitrage — where users migrate from regulated custodial services to unregulated non-custodial ones — is driving explosive growth in Bitcoin-only wallet adoption, P2P exchanges, and self-custody infrastructure across Europe.

This trend directly benefits Bitcoin-only hardware wallets, multisig solutions, and open-source wallet software.

- Self-custody will become the default standard for Bitcoin holdings above threshold amounts, with custodial services reserved for trading and derivatives
