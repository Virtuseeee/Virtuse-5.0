## Conflict Zones

### Conflict 1: Trezor Revenue
- **Dim03/Dim04**: $47.2M (2025)
- **Dim11**: $6.7M (GetLatka data, possibly outdated)
- **Resolution**: Likely $47.2M is correct for 2025; $6.7M appears to be older or partial data. GetLatka noted as potentially outdated. Use $47.2M with MEDIUM confidence.

### Conflict 2: European Hardware Wallet Market Size
- **Dim02**: $204M (2025)
- **Dim04**: $128-204M range (methodology variance)
- **Dim03**: $80-135M (2024 data)
- **Resolution**: Use $150-200M as conservative 2025 range. Variance reflects different year data and methodology (end-user vs wholesale).

### Conflict 3: Total European Crypto Users
- **Dim01**: 50M EU (2024, Coincub)
- **Dim01**: 31M Europe (2024, NewTrading)
- **Dim01**: 218M forecast (2025, NewTrading — likely aggressive)
- **Resolution**: 50M for EU specifically appears most reliable. 31M may exclude some countries. Use 50M EU + UK/Switzerland = ~55-60M total.
