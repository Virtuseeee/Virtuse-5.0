Bitcoin Returns 2013 month by month

Bitcoin's return in each month of 2013, the price at the start and end of the year, its high, low and deepest fall from a high, and how 2013 compares with 2012.

Return 2013+5,286%
End of 2012$13.55
End of 2013$730
Max drawdown−71.2%to 6 Jul 2013
Months higher8/12

Bitcoin rose 5,286% in 2013, from $13.55 at the end of 2012 to $730 at the end of 2013. 8 of 12 months closed higher; the best was November (+451%), the worst December (−34.8%). The deepest fall during the year was −71.2%, from the high of 9 Apr 2013 to 6 Jul 2013; the highest close was $1,135 on 4 Dec 2013. That ranks 1st of 15 full years since 2011, after +187% in 2012.
