Months higher9/12

Bitcoin rose 155% in 2023, from $16,524 at the end of 2022 to $42,217 at the end of 2023. 9 of 12 months closed higher; the best was January (+40.0%), the worst August (−11.2%). The deepest fall during the year was −20.0%, from the high of 13 Jul 2023 to 11 Sep 2023; the highest close was $44,205 on 8 Dec 2023. That ranks 6th of 15 full years since 2011, after −64.4% in 2022.
