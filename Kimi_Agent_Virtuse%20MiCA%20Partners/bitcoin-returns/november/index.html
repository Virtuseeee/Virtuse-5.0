November closed higher in 10 of 16 years (2010–2025), more often than the average month (57% of all months since August 2010). The average of +34.4% is lifted by 2013 (+451%); the median of +8.5% is closer to a typical November. By median it ranks 4th of the 12 months, by average 1st. Last time, in 2025, it returned −17.3%.
