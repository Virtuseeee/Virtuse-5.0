How it works. Four steps from loan request to repayment.

Step 01

Set loan terms

Choose the amount, interest rate and duration, or opt for an instant loan at a predefined rate.

Step 02

Match with an investor

Your request is listed on the marketplace. An investor funds your loan, with no central lender involved.

Step 03

Lock Bitcoin in multi-sig escrow

Send Bitcoin to an on-chain multi-sig escrow address. You never give up your private keys.

Step 04

Receive funds and repay

The loan is sent to your bank account. Repay at maturity and your Bitcoin is returned to your wallet.

Two ways to participate. Borrow against Bitcoin, or fund loans secured by it.

Borrowers

Use your Bitcoin as collateral to borrow fiat (EUR, CHF, CZK) or stablecoins (USDC). Keep your Bitcoin exposure and access liquidity without selling. LTV up to 50%, terms from 3 to 24 months, rates from 5% p.a.

Risks and questions. Read these before you borrow.

What happens if Bitcoin's price falls?

If the value of your collateral falls, you may need to add Bitcoin or the collateral may be liquidated to repay the loan, as set out in Firefish's terms. Check the exact thresholds on Firefish before you borrow. Firefish terms and loan details ↗

Who holds my Bitcoin?

Your Bitcoin is locked in an on-chain multi-sig escrow. Virtuse never holds coins, keys or money; Firefish acts as a technology facilitator between borrowers and investors.
