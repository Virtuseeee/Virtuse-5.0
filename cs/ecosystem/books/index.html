Ekosystém · Knihy

Knihy o Bitcoinu. Co číst dál.

Knihy, které doporučujeme nejčastěji, od historie peněz po fungování protokolu. Ke každé přidáváme hodnocení na jeden řádek, pro koho je, abyste si snadno vybrali další četbu.

8 doporučení · naposledy zkontrolováno 22. října 2026

Naše doporučení. Každé s hodnocením na jeden řádek.

Economics · Beginner

The Bitcoin Standard (Saifedean Ammous)

The classic case for Bitcoin as sound money, told through the history of money. Opinionated and influential.

saifedean.comWeb
Economics · History

Broken Money (Lyn Alden)

A calm, well-researched history of money and banking that ends with Bitcoin. The best pick for sceptical readers.

lynalden.comWeb
Economics

The Fiat Standard (Saifedean Ammous)

The companion to The Bitcoin Standard: how the current money system works and what it costs.

saifedean.comWeb
Economics · Short read

Layered Money (Nik Bhatia)

A short, clear explanation of how money is built in layers, from gold to central banks to Bitcoin and Lightning.

layeredmoney.comWeb
Economics · Technology

The Price of Tomorrow (Jeff Booth)

Why technology pushes prices down while the monetary system fights it. Not a Bitcoin book first, but a strong argument for one.

thepriceoftomorrow.comWeb
Investment thesis

The Bullish Case for Bitcoin (Vijay Boyapati)

The famous essay expanded into a book: why Bitcoin could become a global store of value, step by step.

bullishcaseforbitcoin.comWeb
Technical · Free · Open source

Mastering Bitcoin (Antonopoulos, Harding)

The standard technical reference for developers and curious engineers. Free to read on GitHub.

github.comWeb
Technical · Free · European author

Grokking Bitcoin (Kalle Rosenbaum)

A gentle, illustrated walk through how Bitcoin works, written by a Swedish developer. Free on GitHub.

github.comWeb
