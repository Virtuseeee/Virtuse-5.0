1. Executive Summary

The Core Principle

Virtuse owns the user relationship and the information layer. Licensed partners own the regulated financial layer. Revenue flows to Virtuse through affiliate commissions, subscription fees, and SaaS tool fees -- never through trading spreads, custody fees, or execution markups.

The Three Red Lines

1. No custody -- Virtuse never holds private keys or funds.

2. No execution -- Virtuse never signs transactions or places orders.

3. No personalised advice -- All content is uniform, never tailored to individual circumstances.

3. CASP Referral Partners (Bitcoin-Only)

How CASP Referrals Work

User clicks Virtuse referral link -- completes KYC on licensed CASP platform -- CASP handles all custody, execution, compliance. Virtuse receives CPA (per funded account) or revenue share (% of trading fees). User onboarding happens entirely on the CASP's platform; Virtuse handles no funds or KYC data.
