Your rights
Your right to withdraw

You have the right to withdraw from this contract within 14 days without giving any reason. The period ends 14 days after the day the contract was concluded, which is the day your payment succeeded.

The right applies even after you have opened, read or downloaded the report. We do not ask you to give it up.

How to withdraw

Use the function on this page. You can also send us a clear statement by email to info@virtuse.com or by post to Virtuse Wealth Management, a.s., Borinka 65, 900 32 Borinka, Slovakia, for example with the model form below; the form is not mandatory. It is enough to send your statement before the period ends.

What happens after you withdraw

We refund everything you paid, the full €14, without undue delay and at the latest 14 days after we receive your statement. We use the same card you paid with, and you pay no fees for the refund.

You do not need to return anything, and you do not pay for anything you have already received.

The 30-day refund is separate

On top of this legal right, you can ask for your money back within 30 days of payment by writing to info@virtuse.com, without giving a reason. This is our own promise and does not limit your right to withdraw.
