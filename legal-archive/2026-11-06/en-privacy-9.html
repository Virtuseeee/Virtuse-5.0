9. Virtuse Verdict and Virtuse CFO
Briefly and truthfully
  • Verdict works through five short steps and fixed rules; no AI model reads your answers. The score is computed from structured answers (ranges and choices you pick), not from free text.
  • For the free Lite score, your answers stay in your browser. Only your email, score, grade, area points and gap ids come to us, so we can send you the email, and we do not keep them afterwards.
  • If you buy the full report, your answers leave your device only then. We keep them (without your email) for up to 24 hours on our Cloudflare store to build the report and its PDF; they are then deleted automatically. Your first name is optional.
  • To protect against abuse, we count requests from your IP address. We never store the IP address in readable form, only as a one-way fingerprint (a hash with a salt that rotates every 24 hours). The counter is deleted automatically after 24 hours.
  • Our hosting provider Cloudflare processes technical data (in particular the IP address) in operational logs, which are deleted automatically after 7 days. We do not write your answers to the logs.
  • Only the grade and the weakest areas go into analytics, never your answers.
  • When you make a purchase, we keep payment data and tax documents for 10 years, as the Accounting Act requires. The order record for a full report bought without an account is deleted 30 days after purchase.
  • The exchange export in the fee audit is processed only in your browser and is not sent to us.
  • Please do not enter other people's names, contact details, account numbers, wallet addresses, health data or other sensitive data. Verdict asks only for ranges and choices; the only free-text field is your optional first name.

Special categories of data
We do not need or ask for data on health, religion, ethnic origin or other special categories (Art. 9 GDPR). Verdict collects only structured answers (ranges and choices) and an optional first name, so there is no free-text field that would invite such data. If you nevertheless include it, it is deleted together with your answers, after 24 hours at the latest.
Transfers to third countries
  1. Cloudflare (USA and global network): the transfer relies on Cloudflare, Inc.'s certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Commission Implementing Decision (EU) 2023/1795) and, in support, on standard contractual clauses.
  2. Stripe (Stripe Payments Europe, Limited, with group entities in the USA): the transfer relies on certification under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses, as set out in Stripe's data processing agreement.
  3. Resend (email service, USA): the transfer takes place on the basis of the EU standard contractual clauses (Commission Decision (EU) 2021/914).
Automated assessment
The score in Verdict is computed by fixed rules, the same for everyone, from the structured answers you choose. It is not a decision with legal effects or a similarly significant effect on you within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR, it is not used to assess creditworthiness, and we do not provide it to third parties.
Your rights
You have the right of access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability, the right to object to processing based on legitimate interest, and the right to withdraw consent at any time. Send requests to info@virtuse.com; we will handle them within one month. Please note: for the Lite score we never receive your answers (they stay in your browser), and full-report answers are deleted automatically after 24 hours, so afterwards we cannot retrieve or provide them; the report itself remains available to you as the delivered PDF. You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov SR (Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic), Hraničná 12, 820 07 Bratislava 27, www.dataprotection.gov.sk, or with the supervisory authority of your country of residence.
Changes
We will inform you about material changes on the website, and CFO users also by email.
