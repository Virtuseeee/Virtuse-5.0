Questions and answers

Is Partner Finder free?

Yes. It is free and needs no registration. Virtuse may earn a referral fee when you sign up with a partner, at no extra cost to you, and it doesn't change the results.

Does Virtuse hold my Bitcoin?

No. You open an account directly with the partner you choose. Virtuse never holds your keys or funds.

Is this investment advice?

No. Partner Finder filters partners by your criteria. It does not tell you what to buy or which partner to pick.

How are licences shown?

Only with a link to the official register and the date we checked it. Partners without a sourced licence carry no licence badge.
