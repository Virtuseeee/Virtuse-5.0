Questions. Before you export anything.

How do I get my Kraken export?

On kraken.com, click your profile icon at the top right and choose Documents. Under Exports, click Create export, choose Ledgers, set a start and end date, pick CSV and click Generate. The file appears in the same list when it is ready. That can take a few minutes or several days, and Kraken does not email you. Choose Ledgers rather than Trades: it includes every fee and your instant buys. If you use Kraken Pro and don't see Documents, look under Statements. Kraken's guide

And from ByBit EU or 21bitcoin?

ByBit EU: export your spot trade history from the Orders section. Longer periods may need several files. 21bitcoin: in the app, open your activity and use the download icon to export all transactions. Exchanges move their menus now and then, so check their help pages if a step looks different.

Is my file uploaded anywhere?

No. The audit reads the file in your browser and sends nothing to Virtuse. You can check it yourself in your browser's network tab.

Do I need an account or a card?

No. The free audit needs neither. You only create an account if you later choose Virtuse CFO.

Which exchanges work at launch?

Kraken, ByBit EU and 21bitcoin. More exchanges follow after launch.

Is this investment or tax advice?

No. The audit shows what you paid and the published fees of licensed partners. What you do with it is your decision. For tax questions, ask a tax adviser.
