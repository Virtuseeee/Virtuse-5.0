Six areas

Six areas. Two groups.

Every plan is scored on the same six areas, in two groups: can a fall hurt you, and are you set up. The area map shows the whole shape in one view.

Each area is scored 0 to 10, so 60 points at most. The score is that sum converted to 100 and rounded to 5: 45 out of 60 is 75. Grade A or B (70 and up) means basics in place, C (55 to 69) a few gaps, D or E (under 55) basics missing. It measures how ready your plan is, not whether Bitcoin is a good buy.

      Tax in Slovakia

      The sale rule in plain words

      Slovakia has no holding-period exemption. A gain on a sale is taxed as income at 19 to 35%, plus health insurance contributions (rate and base depend on your situation). Every sale and every swap between coins counts. Gains go into the type B return, due 31 March.

      Indicative, as shown in the Virtuse Tax Agent (Q3 2026). Check with a tax adviser before you file.
