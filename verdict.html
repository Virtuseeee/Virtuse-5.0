Six areas

Six areas. Two groups.

Every plan is scored on the same six areas, in two groups: can a fall hurt you, and are you set up. The area map shows the whole shape in one view.

Each area is scored 0 to 10; the score is their sum, shown out of 100 and rounded to 5. Grade A or B (70 and up) means basics in place, C (55 to 69) a few gaps, D or E basics missing. It measures how ready your plan is, not whether Bitcoin is a good buy.

      Plain-English summary

      Anna, your plan has a sound size. €20,000 is 13% of your savings: after a 70% fall €6,000 would be left, which would hurt but not break your finances. You have no debts besides the mortgage, and you said you would wait in a fall rather than sell.

      Three details are open, and two of them are the ones you worry about. In Slovakia every sale and every swap between coins is taxable, so records matter from the first purchase. With own keys on a first purchase, the backup is the part that most often goes wrong. And your cash reserve is 3 to 6 months; 6 gives a fall less power over you.

      Tax in Slovakia

      The sale rule in plain words

      Slovakia has no holding-period exemption. A gain on a sale is taxed as income at 19 to 35%, plus 16% health insurance contributions. Every sale and every swap between coins counts. Gains go into the type B return, due 31 March.

      Indicative, as shown in the Virtuse Tax Agent (Q4 2026). Check with a tax adviser before you file.
