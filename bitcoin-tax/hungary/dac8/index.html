Your exchange is reporting your Bitcoin trades: DAC8 in Hungary

Since 1 January 2026, crypto exchanges record the identity, tax ID and trades of Hungarian residents under DAC8 for NAV. Your 2026 return is due on 20 May 2027, and NAV's draft return will not include crypto. Gains are taxed at 15%; swapping one crypto-asset for another is not taxed.

What DAC8 is

DAC8 (EU Directive 2023/2226) brings crypto into the automatic exchange of tax information that already covers bank accounts. Hungary implemented it on time with Act LXXXVI of 2025, which amends the act on international administrative cooperation in taxes. Providers' duties apply from 1 January 2026.

Exchanges licensed in other EU countries report to their own tax authority, which sends the data to Hungary. Providers reporting in Hungary register with and report to the National Tax and Customs Administration (NAV).

What your exchange reports

Moving Bitcoin between your own wallets is not taxed, but a transfer out of an exchange shows up in the report. Keep a record that the address is yours.

NAV's draft return does not contain crypto income. If you had taxable crypto income, you have to add it yourself.

How Bitcoin is taxed in Hungary

  • Income from crypto transactions is separately taxed income, taxed at 15% personal income tax. No social contribution tax is charged on top.
  • Tax arises when you receive money, goods or services for crypto. Swapping one crypto-asset for another is not taxed.
  • Your income for the year is total revenue minus documented costs in the same year: purchase price, fees and documented mining costs.
  • Losses from the tax year and the two preceding years can reduce the tax.
  • There is no holding-period exemption.
  • Small transactions need not be declared if each is no more than 10% of the monthly minimum wage, you have no other such revenue that day, and they total no more than one monthly minimum wage in the year.

What to do now

  1. Export your full trade history from every exchange you have used, including closed accounts.
  2. Keep invoices and statements for every purchase and fee: only documented costs are deductible.
  3. Add your crypto income to NAV's draft return by 20 May 2027.
  4. If you did not declare income in earlier years, talk to a tax advisor about a self-revision return (önellenőrzés).
  5. When you withdraw to your own wallet, keep proof that the address belongs to you.

FAQ

Do I pay tax when I swap Bitcoin for another crypto-asset?

No. In Hungary tax arises only when you receive money, goods or services for crypto.

Is my hardware wallet reported?

No. Your wallet's balance is not reported, but transfers from an exchange to it are.

Is social contribution tax due on crypto gains?

No. Since 2022 crypto income is taxed at 15% personal income tax only.

Can I offset crypto losses?

Yes. Losses from the tax year and the two preceding years can reduce the tax on crypto income.
