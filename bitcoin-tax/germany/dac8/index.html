Your exchange is reporting your Bitcoin trades: DAC8 in Germany

Since 1 January 2026, crypto exchanges serving EU residents record your identity, tax ID and trades under DAC8. German providers report 2026 data to the Federal Central Tax Office (BZSt) by 31 July 2027; data from exchanges in other EU countries reaches Germany by 30 September 2027. Bitcoin held over one year stays tax-free.

What DAC8 is

DAC8 (EU Directive 2023/2226) brings crypto into the automatic exchange of tax information that already covers bank accounts. In Germany it is implemented by the Crypto-Asset Tax Transparency Act (KStTG).

Every crypto service provider serving EU residents collects the data, whether it is licensed in Germany or elsewhere in the EU. It reports to the tax authority of its own country, which forwards the data to your country of residence.

How Bitcoin is taxed in Germany

  • Selling Bitcoin is a private sale under section 23 of the Income Tax Act (EStG).
  • Held more than one year: tax-free, whatever the amount.
  • Held one year or less: taxed at your personal income tax rate, up to 45% plus any solidarity surcharge and church tax.
  • Short-term gains below €1,000 a year in total are tax-free. This is a threshold, not an allowance: at €1,000 or more, the whole gain is taxed.
  • Swapping Bitcoin for another crypto-asset within the first year is a taxable sale.
  • Coins bought first count as sold first (FIFO), so you need the date of every purchase.
  • Losses only offset gains from private sales and can be carried forward.
  • Gains go on Annex SO (Anlage SO) of your tax return.

What to do now

  1. Export your full trade history from every exchange you have used, including closed accounts.
  2. Work out the holding period of each purchase. It decides whether you owe 0% or up to 45%.
  3. Use tax software that produces a German report for Annex SO.
  4. If you did not declare earlier gains, see a tax advisor before 2027. You must correct a return once you know it is wrong (section 153 AO), and a voluntary disclosure (section 371 AO) only protects you before the tax office finds the gap.
  5. When you withdraw to your own wallet, keep proof that the address belongs to you.

FAQ

Does DAC8 apply to exchanges outside the EU?

DAC8 covers every provider serving EU residents, including ones based outside the EU. Many non-EU countries are adopting the OECD's parallel standard (CARF) on a similar timeline.

Is my hardware wallet reported?

No. Your wallet's balance is not reported, but transfers from an exchange to it are.

Do I owe tax on Bitcoin I have not sold?

No. In Germany tax is due when you sell or swap, not while you hold.

I bought before 2026. Does that matter?

DAC8 data starts with 2026, but the tax office can still ask about earlier years within the normal assessment period.
