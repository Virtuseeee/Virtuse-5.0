Bitcoin tax in Germany

Bitcoin tax in Germany as of Q3 2026: Up to 45 % income tax (progressive). Exemption: 0 % after 1-year holding (private-sale exemption). Filing: Annual tax return. Crypto-to-crypto is taxable within the first year. FIFO record-keeping is critical. Indicative 2026 overview, not tax advice.
