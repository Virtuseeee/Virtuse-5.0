Your exchange is reporting your Bitcoin trades: DAC8 in Bulgaria

Bulgaria adopted DAC8 in September 2026, late but retroactive to 1 January 2026, so exchanges must report the whole year. Providers send 2026 data to the National Revenue Agency by 30 June 2027. Your annual return is due on 30 April 2027, and crypto gains are taxed at a flat 10%.

What DAC8 is

DAC8 (EU Directive 2023/2226) brings crypto into the automatic exchange of tax information that already covers bank accounts. Bulgaria implemented it by amending the Tax and Social Insurance Procedure Code, promulgated in September 2026, more than eight months after the EU deadline. It still applies from 1 January 2026.

Exchanges licensed in other EU countries report to their own tax authority, which forwards the data to Bulgaria. Providers reporting in Bulgaria send it to the National Revenue Agency (NRA) directly.

What your exchange reports

ReportedDetails
Who you areName, address, date of birth and tax identification number
Crypto to euro or other moneyTotal amounts bought and sold per crypto-asset and year
Crypto to cryptoTotal amounts per crypto-asset
TransfersTransfers out of the exchange, including to your own wallet

Moving Bitcoin between your own wallets is not taxed, but a transfer out of an exchange shows up in the report. Keep a record that the address is yours.

Because the law came late, your exchange may still ask you to confirm your tax details. Customers who do not complete these checks can be blocked from trading.

The data arrives after the filing deadline. A missing gain can surface months after you file.

How Bitcoin is taxed in Bulgaria

  • The National Revenue Agency treats gains from selling or exchanging crypto as income from the transfer of financial assets.
  • The rate is the flat 10% personal income tax.
  • The taxable result is your yearly total: proceeds minus purchase costs, with gains and losses in the same year offset against each other.
  • There is no holding-period exemption.
  • Exchanging one crypto-asset for another is generally treated as a disposal.
  • Gains go in an annex to the annual tax return, due by 30 April.
  • Trading as a business or mining can be taxed as business income instead; ask a tax advisor.

What to do now

  1. Export your full trade history from every exchange you have used, including closed accounts.
  2. Respond to any request from your exchange to confirm your tax details.
  3. Work out your yearly net result and declare it by 30 April 2027.
  4. If you did not declare gains in earlier years, talk to a tax advisor about correcting them before the NRA receives the data.
  5. When you withdraw to your own wallet, keep proof that the address belongs to you.

FAQ

Bulgaria passed DAC8 late. Is 2026 still reported?

Yes. The law applies retroactively from 1 January 2026, so exchanges report the full year.

Is my hardware wallet reported?

No. Your wallet's balance is not reported, but transfers from an exchange to it are.

Does it help to hold Bitcoin longer?

No. Bulgaria has no holding-period exemption; gains are taxed at 10% whenever you sell.

I bought before 2026. Does that matter?

DAC8 data starts with 2026, but the NRA can still assess earlier years within the statutory period.
