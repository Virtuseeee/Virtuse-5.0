Your exchange is reporting your Bitcoin trades: DAC8 in Slovenia

Since 1 January 2026, crypto exchanges record the identity, tax ID and trades of Slovenian residents under DAC8, and Slovenian providers report to FURS by 31 May 2027. For now, private gains from selling Bitcoin are not taxed in Slovenia; a proposed 25% tax was withdrawn in 2025. Business trading and mining are taxed.

What DAC8 is

DAC8 (EU Directive 2023/2226) brings crypto into the automatic exchange of tax information that already covers bank accounts. Slovenia implemented it on time by amending the Tax Procedure Act (ZDavP-2P, Official Gazette 100/2025), applicable from 1 January 2026.

Providers reporting in Slovenia send the data to the Financial Administration of the Republic of Slovenia (FURS). Exchanges licensed in other EU countries report to their own tax authority, which sends the data to Slovenia.

What your exchange reports

ReportedDetails
Who you areName, address, date of birth and tax identification number
Crypto to moneyTotal amounts bought and sold per crypto-asset and year
Crypto to cryptoTotal amounts per crypto-asset
TransfersTransfers out of the exchange, including to your own wallet

Moving Bitcoin between your own wallets is not taxed, but a transfer out of an exchange shows up in the report. Keep a record that the address is yours.

The reports let FURS see who trades how much. That matters most if your trading could count as a business activity, or if Slovenia introduces a crypto gains tax later.

How Bitcoin is taxed in Slovenia

  • Gains from selling crypto as a private individual are not subject to personal income tax, as long as the trading is not a business activity.
  • Trading as a business activity is taxed as business income on the progressive scale, plus social contributions.
  • Mining income is taxed as other income, with a 25% advance tax and no deduction for costs.
  • The government proposed a 25% tax on crypto disposals in 2025. The bill was withdrawn from Parliament in November 2025 and is not in force. If it returns, its rules and start date may differ from the 2025 draft.

What to do now

  1. Keep your full trade history from every exchange, including closed accounts, in case a future tax needs your purchase costs.
  2. If you trade often or at large scale, ask a tax advisor whether FURS could treat it as a business activity.
  3. If you mine, declare the mining income.
  4. Watch for news on the crypto gains tax: a new law could start with a valuation date.
  5. When you withdraw to your own wallet, keep proof that the address belongs to you.

FAQ

Do I pay tax when I sell Bitcoin in Slovenia?

Not as a private individual under current law. Business trading and mining are taxed.

Why is my exchange still reporting me?

DAC8 applies to all EU residents regardless of whether their country taxes the gains. FURS receives the data either way.

Is the 25% crypto tax coming?

It was proposed in 2025 and withdrawn in November 2025. It is not in force; any new version needs a new law.

Is my hardware wallet reported?

No. Your wallet's balance is not reported, but transfers from an exchange to it are.
