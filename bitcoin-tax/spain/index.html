Bitcoin tax in Spain

Updated 13 Oct 2026

Bitcoin tax in Spain as of Q3 2026: 19 to 30% progressive tax on savings income. Exemption: No holding-period exemption. Filing: Annual IRPF return; Modelo 721 for crypto held with custodians abroad over €50,000. Crypto-to-crypto swaps are taxable disposals. Use FIFO for identical coins. As of Q3 2026, checked 13 Oct 2026. Not tax advice.
