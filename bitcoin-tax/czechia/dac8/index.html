DAC8 in Czechia: your Bitcoin trades are being recorded

Czechia has not yet passed its DAC8 law: the bill is still in Parliament. The government intends 2026 as the first reporting year anyway, and exchanges in other EU countries already record Czech residents' trades. Bitcoin held over three years, or sales under CZK 100,000 a year, stay tax-free.

What DAC8 is

DAC8 (EU Directive 2023/2226) brings crypto into the automatic exchange of tax information that already covers bank accounts. Member states had to adopt it by the end of 2025.

Czechia missed that deadline. A government bill amending the Act on International Cooperation in Tax Administration (Act 164/2013) has been in the Chamber of Deputies since February 2026. The Ministry of Finance intends to keep 1 January 2026 as the start of data collection, so 2026 would still be the first reporting year.

Exchanges licensed in other EU countries record Czech customers under their own countries' laws and report to their own tax authority, which sends the data to Czechia. Under the bill, providers reporting in Czechia would send it to the Specialised Tax Office.

How Bitcoin is taxed in Czechia

  • Income from selling crypto is other income under section 10 of the Income Tax Act, taxed at 15%, or 23% on the part of your total tax base above 36 times the average wage.
  • Since February 2025 two exemptions apply. Time test: a sale is tax-free if you held the coins for more than three years. Value test: sales are tax-free if your total proceeds in the year are CZK 100,000 or less; the limit is on proceeds, not profit.
  • The time-test exemption is reduced if exempt crypto income exceeds CZK 40 million in a year. Exempt income above CZK 5 million must still be reported in the return.
  • Swapping one crypto-asset for another is generally treated as a taxable disposal.
  • You can deduct the purchase price and fees, but only up to the income: a loss cannot reduce other income or be carried forward.
  • No social or health insurance is charged on this income.
  • Crypto income goes in Annex 2 of the personal income tax return.

What to do now

  1. Export your full trade history from every exchange you have used, including closed accounts.
  2. Note the purchase date of every coin: the three-year time test depends on it.
  3. Add up your gross proceeds for the year to see whether the CZK 100,000 value test covers you.
  4. If you did not declare taxable gains in earlier years, talk to a tax advisor about a supplementary return.
  5. When you withdraw to your own wallet, keep proof that the address belongs to you.

FAQ

Czechia has no DAC8 law yet. Am I reported?

Exchanges in other EU countries already record your trades and must send them to Czechia. Czech providers will report once the bill passes, and it is meant to cover 2026.

Is Bitcoin tax-free after three years?

Yes. Since February 2025 a sale is exempt if you held the coins for more than three years, up to CZK 40 million of exempt income a year.

Is my hardware wallet reported?

No. Your wallet's balance is not reported, but transfers from an exchange to it are.

Do small sales need to be taxed?

No, if your total crypto sale proceeds in the year are CZK 100,000 or less.
