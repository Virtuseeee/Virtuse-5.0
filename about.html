How we work

Bitcoin only

We cover Bitcoin services only. No altcoins and no tokens.

Your funds stay with you

We don't hold client funds or keys and we don't execute orders. Every service runs on the partner's own platform.

Fixed rules, not commission

Partner Finder orders partners by fixed rules based on your answers, not by the referral fees some partners pay us.

Our name

What is Virtuse?

Our name is inspired by virtù, a concept introduced by Niccolò Machiavelli. To him, virtù wasn't about conventional morality. It was about strength, control and the ability to shape your own circumstances.

We see Bitcoin in the same way: money you can hold and control yourself, without asking anyone's permission.

That is the idea behind the name.

How we look at partners

Before a partner appears on Virtuse, we look at the same points. They help you compare. They are not a guarantee.

Licence or registration

Which licence or registration the partner holds and in which register it is listed.

Custody and withdrawals

Whether you can withdraw your Bitcoin to your own wallet and who holds it while it is on the platform.

Track record

How long the partner has operated and what is publicly known about its history.

Fees

Whether fees are published and how they compare with other partners.
