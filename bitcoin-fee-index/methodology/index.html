Fee Index · Methodology

How the Fee Index is calculated

Fees checked 12 Oct 2026. Every number links to the partner page it comes from.

Listed fees

  • 21bitcoin: Auto-Invest savings plan: 0% service fee from day 8; the first 7 days carry the regular 0.99% (0.79% with a referral code). Instant buy (Smash Buy) 1.49%. Source, checked 12 Oct 2026.
  • Bybit EU: Spot trading fee 0.25% at VIP 0, maker and taker alike (bybit.eu fee page, new fee structure for trades from 5 Oct 2026; page last updated 5 Oct 2026, checked on bybit.eu 12 Oct 2026, 14:16 UTC). Source, checked 12 Oct 2026.
  • Kraken Pro: Kraken Pro spot, Tier 1 ($0+ 30-day volume): 0.40% maker, 0.80% taker. Instant Buy in the Kraken app is a separate 1% fee plus a spread. Source, checked 12 Oct 2026.

The ranking uses the fee for the route a monthly buyer would use: 21bitcoin's Auto-Invest plan, a spot market buy on Bybit EU, and a spot market buy (taker) on Kraken Pro at the entry tier.

Estimated spread

  • Bybit EU and Kraken Pro: we simulate a market buy of each amount (€50, €100, €250, €500, €1,000) against the public BTC/EUR order book and compare the average fill price with the mid price (halfway between the best bid and the best ask). That covers half the bid-ask spread plus any slippage. Average of samples taken at 2026-10-12T12:26:11Z, 2026-10-12T12:26:27Z, 2026-10-12T12:26:42Z (UTC).
  • 21bitcoin: there is no public order book. 21bitcoin buy price shown in the app before confirming, against Kraken BTC/EUR mid price (1-minute OHLC close) at the same minute. Not measured yet: until then 21bitcoin is listed without a rank.

Spreads move with the market. A spread is an estimate for the time it was measured, not a promise of the price you will get.

Total cost

  • Total cost per buy = listed fee + estimated spread, as a share of the amount.
  • Per year = 12 × monthly amount × total cost.
  • Ranking: lowest total cost first, for each amount separately. No partner has a fixed place, and a referral fee never changes the order.
  • Not included: network and withdrawal fees, currency conversion from CZK, PLN, HUF, RON or BGN, and promotions that are not on the partner's fee page.

Questions and answers

Where do the fees come from?

From each partner's own fee page, checked on 12 Oct 2026. The links are in the list above.

Why include the spread?

A route can charge no fee and still sell Bitcoin above the market price. Adding the spread makes routes comparable.

How often is this updated?

Every quarter, and whenever a partner changes its published fees. Past quarters stay online as archive pages, so citations keep working.

Not investment advice. Virtuse never holds your keys or funds. KYC and onboarding happen on each partner's regulated platform.
