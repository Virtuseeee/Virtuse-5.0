Bitcoin Fee Index methodology

Methodology as of Q3 2026: rank partners by annual fee drag at a stated monthly euro contribution. Formula is the live stacking module’s: percentage of each purchase plus allocated monthly subscription. Spreads, FX and miner fees are out of scope. RevenueBot’s rate is marked illustrative in the source schedule. Not a brokerage quote.

Formula

costPerPurchase = contribution * pct + fixed + monthly / (periodsPerYear / 12)
annualDrag = costPerPurchase * periodsPerYear

Default periodsPerYear = 12. Same as the live stacking module; not re-derived.

Out of scope

  • Bid/ask spread
  • FX from CZK, PLN, HUF, RON, BGN into EUR
  • Bitcoin network miner fees
  • Promotional discounts that are not in the schedule

FAQ

Where do the percentages come from?

From the shipped Stacking Strategist FEE_SCHEDULE (21bitcoin 0%, ByBit EU 0.1%, Kraken 0.16%, RevenueBot 0.4% + €4/month illustrative). Numbers are not edited for SEO.

What is the annual-drag formula?

costPerPurchase = contribution × pct + fixed + monthly / (periodsPerYear / 12); annualDrag = costPerPurchase × periodsPerYear. Copied from the live stacking module.

Why is a later Banxa/CASP table not used?

A brief placeholder schedule existed for early prototypes. Indexable pages use the live module partners so Google does not rank invented brand names.

How often will this change?

When the stacking fee schedule is updated, change seo-build/data/fee-schedule-live.json and re-run npm run build. Archive URLs stay stable.
